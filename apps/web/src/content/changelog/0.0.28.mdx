- Put a filter over the whole picture — CRT, LCD, VHS, film, halftone, fish eye, glow and four more.
- Each look has its own controls, they are set per clip, and anything that moves starts switched off.
- Add more footage to a recording you already made: press Add Recording under the preview, or R.
- Drag clips into a different order. Zooms travel with the footage they were placed over.
- A title stays the length you set it, and keeps its entrance and exit when you move it.
- Drag titles anywhere on their row, past each other, or onto another row.
- Closing the window you were recording, or unplugging a display, no longer breaks the take. Recordings already damaged by this cannot be recovered.
- The stray blue outline around controls in the recording panel is gone.
