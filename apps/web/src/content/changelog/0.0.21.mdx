- Cursor shadows have controls for opacity, blur and offset. New projects start
  with a restrained 20% opacity, 1% blur and 1% offset.
- A still cursor shadow exports cleanly now. Metal no longer turns the padded
  area around a static pointer into a black rectangle, so the export matches
  the preview.
- A new hand. The pointer drawn over a link is a proper hand now, with fingers, in the same two tones as the arrows; the resize pointers are redrawn to match. Every recording gets it, including ones made before the update.
- The strip of screen just above the dock takes clicks again. The window stands taller than the pill to give the tooltips somewhere to go, and it used to swallow anything you clicked there.
- Text over the recording. Press T, click Add Text, or click and drag on the new row above the clips, and a title lands where the playhead is. Eight looks to start from — a title, a title with a line under it, a lower third, a callout, a section, a quote, a typewriter and a word-by-word — each with its own way in and out, and you can change any of it: the words of every line on their own, the font, weight, size, colour, capitals, spacing, an outline, a shadow, a plate behind the words, and how it arrives and leaves. Drag it around the preview to place it; pull a corner to scale it.
- A text can sit over another. When a row holds a text, a second row appears above it, up to five. Drag a bar between rows to move it; hold ⌥ while dragging to leave a copy. A bar reads what its text says, so a row of them can be told apart.
- The text is in the export exactly as it is in the preview, whatever size you export at.
- A pointer that streaks in the preview now streaks in the export too. The blur was drawn on screen and dropped from the file.
- The layout picker is shorter. Each arrangement's card is as tall as the picture inside it, with the same room on every side.
- Switching to another panel in the side bar opens it at the top. It used to open wherever the last panel had been scrolled to.
