- The camera and microphone choosers in the dock are macOS menus. They open above the panel, fully drawn, every time — they used to open blank now and then, or at the wrong size for a moment before settling.
- The permissions warning opens the same way. Each missing permission is a line with what it costs underneath and an Allow beside it, and there is a Restart Prequel item when macOS only reads the grant at launch.
- The chevron beside a device name turns to point at its menu while the menu is open.
- A share link opens in a player of Prequel's own rather than the browser's. The keyboard works from the moment the page opens — space to play, J and L to jump, the arrows to nudge, a number to jump to a tenth, and `?` for the rest — and there is a speed control, picture in picture and full screen.
- A shared recording has chapters. They are written from the transcript when you press Share, drawn on the scrub bar and listed beside the video, and a click on one goes there. Nothing to do on your side: any recording that was transcribed gets them.
- A shared recording has subtitles, off until the viewer turns them on. Made from the same transcript, so a recording exported without captions can still be read along with.
- A pointer that stops now stays put. It used to creep, very slowly, towards wherever it went next for as long as it rested — in the preview and in the export — and then set off from somewhere it had never been. Every recording gets this, not just new ones.
