- Captions can be set in a face of your own: Helvetica Neue, Avenir Next, Futura, Georgia, Menlo and the rest of what your Mac already has.
- Blur in is what a new recording starts with.
- The inspector is rebuilt. Every control is one bar the same height with its own icon, sliders carry their name and reading inside the track, and a switch is a row you can press anywhere rather than a target the size of a thumbnail.
- Panels are grouped, so Border, Shadow, Motion and Hiding each say what they are instead of leaving eight sliders in a column.
- Colours are picked in the app rather than in the system panel, and the picker sits under the swatch it belongs to.
- The timeline says which moment you are pointing at, not only which one you are on.
- Playing selects the clip you are watching, and pressing a clip you are already editing moves the playhead to where you pressed rather than back to its start.
- The app opens faster, and the recordings list no longer waits for the editor before it draws.
- Presets. Dress a recording the way you like it, save the look, and put it on the next one — the arrangement, the background, the captions, the camera, the frame it was made in, and the way a zoom moves.
- A logo of your own, anywhere in the frame, dragged where you want it and sized by its corner. It travels with a preset.
- The camera takes a border and a roundness of its own, set where the camera is rather than in the frame around the recording.
- The background can be thrown out of focus, so a busy wallpaper sits back and the recording reads.
- Ten more pointers, for when a hammer or a frog says it better than an arrow does.
- Guides while you drag. Both pictures snap to the middle of the frame, and the camera to the corners.
- Press a picture in the preview and its panel opens — the camera, the recording, or the captions.
- Right-click the timeline to split a clip where you pointed, remove one, or copy a zoom.
- Give every clip the settings of the one in front of you, from any panel.
- Choose a background of your own twice and you get the second one. It used to keep showing the first.
- Stopping a recording opens its editor rather than the list of recordings.
- SVGs work wherever an image does, and stay sharp at any size you export.
