- A clip can play back at a different speed. Select one on the timeline and the Recording panel has a new Speed section: drag the slider from 0.25x to 4x, or pick one of the five stops underneath it.
- The preview and the export agree. A clip played faster or slower shows it in both, and its audio's pitch rises or falls with it rather than staying level.
- The control is grey with nothing selected. Speed belongs to a clip, not to the whole recording, so there is nothing for it to set until you choose one.
