- Prequel no longer bakes the pointer into a recording, and the setting that did has gone. It traded everything the Cursor panel can do — restyling, resizing, smoothing, the lean, motion blur, hiding, zooming to the pointer — for a pointer that matched its shape slightly more closely, and it could not be undone once a take was made.
- If you had it switched on, it is off from the next launch. Recordings you already made with it on are unchanged and still open; the pointer in those is part of the picture, so there is nothing to restyle.
- Every keyboard and mouse sound has a play button now. Open the Audio panel, open the Keyboard or Mouse clicks menu, and press play on a row to hear that one without choosing it. A keyboard plays a few seconds of typing, a mouse plays a single click.
- They play while the sounds are switched off, so you can listen through the whole list before you turn anything on.
- A recording made with the microphone on could come out unopenable — no preview in the editor, no thumbnail in the library, and nothing to say why. One track failing to close at the end of a take was taking the others with it, and the files it left behind were never finished. New recordings are fine; ones already in that state cannot be recovered, because what is missing was never written.
- Split and delete now sit at the left end of the editor's playback row, ahead of the timecode, rather than over on the right. Undo still appears beside them the moment there is a step back to take.
