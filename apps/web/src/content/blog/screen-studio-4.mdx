## What is Screen Studio 4?

Screen Studio 4 is the fourth major version of Screen Studio, the macOS screen
recorder that zooms in on your clicks and your typing and smooths the cursor
as it records. It arrived on 2 October 2026. The headline feature is an
upscaler that sharpens a recording up to 6.5 times its size, on the Mac.

The price did not move. Screen Studio is still $29 a month, or $9 a month
billed yearly, which is $108 up front. There is no free tier and no one-time
licence; that was withdrawn in October 2025.

Screen Studio made this category, so a new major version gets attention. It
is also the first one in a long while. Through 2026 the app shipped point
releases: 3.5.2 in January with an iPhone 17 Pro Max mockup, 3.6 in February
with fixes to microphone selection and export length, 3.7 in May, and its
fifth patch in August. Then two quiet months, and 4.0.

This post covers what is in it, what is not, and what the rest of the
category shipped in the meantime.

## What is new in Screen Studio 4

### An upscaler trained on the Mac's own interface

The problem it solves is real. A zoom enlarges pixels, and text on a 1080p
capture goes soft by the time it fills the frame at 2x. Screen Studio's answer
is a model of its own. Adam Pietrasiak, the founder, described how it was
trained in a thread on X on launch day: a bot generated random SwiftUI apps
that look like macOS, screenshotted them, shrank the screenshots, and taught
the model to put the detail back. Around 5,000 of them.

It runs on your Mac, so nothing is uploaded, and the claim is up to 6.5x.
If your recordings are made on a small display and zoomed hard, it is the
part of 4.0 you will notice.

### The rest of the release

The announcement leads with the upscaler, and so does the founder's thread.
The zooms, the cursor, the camera layouts and the export are the Screen
Studio you already know. Nothing in the launch changes the subscription,
the 4K 60 export ceiling, or the 30-minute cap and branded page on a share
link.

That is the honest summary of 4.0: one well-made feature, after a year of
fixes and mockups, at the same $29 a month.

## What the category shipped while Screen Studio was on 3.7

Most of what makes a screen recording look produced in 2026 has been
available elsewhere for months. Cap's 0.6 release on 15 September added 3D
scenes with perspective and focus blur, and a camera cut out of its
background. Prequel shipped 30 releases between 28 August and 2 October.
These are the ones you can see in the video.

### A loupe over the work

![Prequel's loupe: a round magnifying glass over the Create menu in Google Calendar, with the rest of the window still readable around it](/blog/screen-studio-4/prequel-loupe.webp)

Set a zoom's **Method** to **Loupe** and a magnifying glass comes down over
what you are doing, while the rest of the screen stays where it is and stays
readable. The glass follows the pointer or holds a region. The **Glass** tab
sets its curvature, colour fringing and reflection, and the picture inside
it smears as it travels, so a lens chasing a quick hand reads as motion.

Under **Sounds** in the **Audio** panel, pick a keyboard (Tactile, Linear,
Clicky, Thock, or the iPhone's own soft tock) and one of ten mouse clicks,
from a barely-there tap to a wooden knock. The export carries a keystroke
for every key you pressed and a click for every click, landing on the frame
where it happens. Prequel makes the sounds itself, so no microphone is
involved, and on the mechanical keyboards no two presses sound quite the
same. Every row has a play button, so you can hear it before you pick it.

CRT, LCD, VHS, film, halftone, fish eye, glow, Dream, Lost signal, Dither,
four retro palettes (Pico-8, Game Boy, C64 and Risograph) and more. Each has its
own controls and is set per clip, so the intro can look like a VHS tape and
the demo can look like a Mac.

### And the rest, since the end of August

- A teleprompter in the notch that follows your voice and never appears in the recording.
- Titles and text over the recording, in eight looks, up to five rows deep.
- Noise reduction on the microphone, Light or Strong.
- Colour looks for the camera, built to flatter skin, with a preview on your own face.
- A camera shape that follows you: a free-form bubble that moves as you move.
- Speed per clip, from 0.25x to 4x.
- Import a video you already have, and caption it from its own sound.
- Shared links with chapters and subtitles written from the transcript.

None of that needed a new major version. It arrived a few days at a time.

## So, is Screen Studio 4 worth it

If you are already subscribed, update. It costs you nothing extra and the
upscaler is good at what it does.

If your renewal is coming up, or you are choosing a recorder today, record
the same take in both and compare the two files. Screen Studio 4 gives you
a sharper zoom. Prequel gives you the zoom, the loupe, the sounds, the
filters, the teleprompter and 4K at 120 fps, for $29 once or $9 a month,
with several releases a week.
