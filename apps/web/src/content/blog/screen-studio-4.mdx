## What is Screen Studio 4?

Screen Studio 4 is the fourth major version of Screen Studio, the macOS screen
recorder that zooms in on your clicks and your typing and smooths the cursor
as it records. It arrived on 2 October 2026, and Screen Studio calls it its
biggest update yet: a glass loupe, a camera cut out of its background, editing
by transcript, split screen, colour grading for the camera and sharper zooms.

The price did not move. Screen Studio is still $29 a month, or $9 a month
billed yearly, which is $108 up front. There is no free tier and no one-time
licence; that was withdrawn in October 2025. Export still tops out at 4K and
60 fps.

It is a real release, and it follows a quiet year. Through 2026 the app
shipped point releases: 3.5.2 in January with an iPhone 17 Pro Max mockup,
3.6 in February with fixes to microphone selection and export length, 3.7 in
May, and its fifth patch in August. Then two months with nothing, and 4.0.

This post covers what is in it, which of it was already shipping elsewhere,
and what is still missing.

## What is new in Screen Studio 4

Read off Screen Studio's own changelog on 3 October 2026.

- **Glass Loupe.** A lens magnifies one detail while the rest of the screen stays in view.
- **Camera cutout, in beta.** Your background is removed on the Mac and you sit in front of the screen.
- **Face tracking and Hide during silence.** The camera keeps your face centred, and hides itself while you are not talking.
- **Colour grading.** Twelve looks for the camera, each previewed on your own picture, plus your own LUT files.
- **Edit by transcript.** Delete a sentence from the transcript to cut it from the video.
- **Split screen.** You and the screen side by side, or stacked in a vertical video, with animated switches between layouts.
- **Sharper zooms.** A model trained on generated macOS screenshots sharpens zoomed-in text, on the Mac.
- **Faster exports.** Up to 3x faster on Apple silicon, and new recordings are up to 2x smaller.
- **Smaller things.** A redesigned ripple on clicks, keyboard shortcuts drawn as real keys, caption fonts and animations, Unsplash backgrounds, a noise reduction mode for the microphone, and a whole project shared as a link, in beta.

Two things to know before you update. Projects saved in version 4 do not
open in version 3. And the changelog warns that zooms in projects made with
version 3 "might show a different part of your recording".

The sharper zooms come from a model the founder, Adam Pietrasiak, described
in a thread on X on launch day: a bot generated random SwiftUI apps that look
like macOS, screenshotted them, shrank the screenshots, and trained the model
to put the detail back. Around 5,000 of them.

### What did not change

The subscription, and the ceiling. It is $29 a month or $108 a year, with
nothing to own. Export is 4K at 60 fps on both plans, a share link stops at
30 minutes and carries Screen Studio's branding, and there is still no team
workspace.

Look at the headline list again with dates next to it. Several of the
features Screen Studio waited for a major version to ship were already in
other recorders by mid-September.

Dates from [Prequel's public changelog](/docs/changelog). Cap's 0.6 release on 15 September
also added a camera cutout, along with 3D scenes with perspective and focus

Screen Studio got there. It took a year of point releases and a version
number to do it, and Prequel shipped [30 releases](/docs/changelog) between 28 August and
2 October. Here is what that looks like.

## What Prequel ships today

### A loupe you can shape

![Prequel's loupe: a round magnifying glass over the Create menu in Google Calendar, with the rest of the window still readable around it](/blog/screen-studio-4/prequel-loupe.webp)

Both apps have a loupe now. In Prequel, set a zoom's **Method** to **Loupe** and a magnifying glass comes down over
what you are doing, while the rest of the screen stays where it is and stays
readable. The glass follows the pointer or holds a region. The **Glass** tab
sets its curvature, colour fringing and reflection, and the picture inside
it smears as it travels, so a lens chasing a quick hand reads as motion.

Under **Sounds** in the **Audio** panel, pick a keyboard (Tactile, Linear,
Clicky, Thock, or the iPhone's own soft tock) and one of ten mouse clicks,
from a barely-there tap to a wooden knock. The export carries a keystroke
for every key you pressed and a click for every click, landing on the frame
where it happens. Prequel makes the sounds itself, so no microphone is
involved, and on the mechanical keyboards no two presses sound quite the
same. Every row has a play button, so you can hear it before you pick it.

Screen Studio has had mouse click sounds for a long time. It has nothing for
the keyboard, so a demo that is mostly typing stays silent.

CRT, LCD, VHS, film, halftone, fish eye, glow, Dream, Lost signal, Dither,
four retro palettes (Pico-8, Game Boy, C64 and Risograph) and more. Each has its
own controls and is set per clip, so the intro can look like a VHS tape and
the demo can look like a Mac. Screen Studio 4 has no filters over the
picture.

### And the rest

- Export at up to 4K and 120 fps. Screen Studio 4 stops at 60.
- A perspective tilt on the push-in, and focus falling away from what the zoom is aimed at.
- Titles and text over the recording, in eight looks, up to five rows deep.
- A teleprompter in the notch that follows your voice as you read and never appears in the recording. Screen Studio's prompter sits in its speaker notes.
- A camera shape that follows you: a free-form bubble that moves as you move.
- Speed per clip, from 0.25x to 4x.
- Import a video you already have, and caption it from its own sound.
- Shared links with chapters and subtitles written from the transcript, and a team workspace.
- A lifetime licence: $29 once.

None of that needed a new major version. It arrived a few days at a time.

## So, is Screen Studio 4 worth it

If you are already subscribed, update. It costs you nothing extra, and it is
the biggest step Screen Studio has taken in a year. Keep a copy of any
version 3 project you care about, since version 4 saves in a format version
3 cannot open.

If your renewal is coming up, or you are choosing a recorder today, record
the same take in both and compare the two files. Screen Studio 4 brings the
loupe, the cutout and the transcript editing up to where Prequel already
was. Prequel adds the typing sounds, the filters, the titles, the tilt and
4K at 120 fps, for $29 once or $9 a month, with several releases a week.
