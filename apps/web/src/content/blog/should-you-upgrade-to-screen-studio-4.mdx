## What is in the Screen Studio 4 update?

Screen Studio 4, released on 2 October 2026, adds a glass loupe, a camera
cut out of its background (in beta), face tracking, colour grading for the
camera with twelve looks and LUT support, editing by transcript, split
screen layouts, sharper zoomed-in text from a model that runs on the Mac,
and exports up to 3x faster.

What it keeps: the $29 monthly or $108 yearly subscription, export at up to
4K and 60 fps, and a 30-minute cap on share links. Projects saved in version
4 do not open in version 3.

## Five questions to ask before you pay for Screen Studio 4

### 1. Were you waiting for the loupe, the cutout or transcript editing?

If those three were why you were looking around, Screen Studio 4 answers
them, and an update you already pay for is the cheapest way to get them.
Know that Prequel has had transcript editing since
[0.0.13](/docs/changelog#v0.0.13) on 6 September and the camera cutout since
[0.0.19](/docs/changelog#v0.0.19) on 13 September, and its loupe shipped in
[0.0.35](/docs/changelog#v0.0.35), the same day as Screen Studio's.

If you wanted something past that, typing sounds, filters, titles over the
recording or a tilt on the zoom, version 4 does not add it.

[Screen recording on a Mac, the complete guide →](/blog/screen-recording-on-mac)

### 2. Do you export at 60 fps, or would 120 look better?

Screen Studio 4 still stops at 4K 60. Prequel exports at up to 4K and 120
fps, in H.264 or HEVC. On a high refresh rate display, a fast scroll or a
cursor flick at 120 reads as smooth where 60 shows steps.

### 3. Does the video need to sound like somebody is using it?

A silent cursor clicking through an app feels like a slideshow. Prequel adds
a keystroke for every key you pressed and a click for every click, made in
the app, so no microphone picks up your desk. Pick Tactile, Linear, Clicky,
Thock or the iPhone keyboard, and one of ten mouse clicks. Each one has a
play button so you can hear it first.

[Product demo videos that hold attention →](/blog/product-demo-videos)

### 4. How often do you want the app to change?

Screen Studio's 2026 releases before 4.0 were 3.5.2 in January, 3.6 in
February, 3.7 in May and 3.7.5 in August, then two quiet months. Prequel
shipped [30 releases](/docs/changelog) from 28 August to 2 October: a loupe, more than 15
filters, keyboard and mouse sounds, a teleprompter, text over the recording,
microphone noise reduction, camera colour looks, per-clip speed and video
import among them.

If you pay monthly, you are paying for what arrives next month as much as
for what is there now. Look at the rate, not just the version number.

You are already subscribed and your renewal is months away. Update: 4.0 is

## Who should try Prequel first

You are choosing a recorder for the first time, your renewal is due, or you
want more from the edit than Screen Studio 4 gives you: sounds on the
typing, a filter on the intro, a title over each step, a prompter that
follows your voice, 120 fps on the way out, and a licence you pay for once.

![Prequel's loupe: a round magnifying glass over the Create menu in Google Calendar, with the rest of the window still readable around it](/blog/screen-studio-4/prequel-loupe.webp)

Prequel opens a recording with the first pass done: zooms on every click
and burst of typing, the camera framed in a corner, a background with
padding and a shadow, the microphone and system audio balanced, and the
cursor smoothed and hidden when it idles. From there, every control is
yours. Zoom method, glass curvature, colour fringing, filter per clip,
keyboard voice, camera shape and look. Export has no watermark on any plan
and no cap on how long a take can be.

The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/haxzie/prequel) under
FSL-1.1-ALv2, which becomes Apache 2.0 two years after each release.

**Price.** $29 once, or $9 a month. Seven days free with the whole app in
it. **Requires.** An Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 14 or later and the Screen
Recording permission.
