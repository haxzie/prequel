## What is Screen Charm?

Screen Charm is a macOS app that records your screen and edits the recording
for you. It captures a display, a window or an area along with the camera,
the microphone and the Mac's own audio, then applies zooms to every click,
smooths the cursor and puts a background behind the window. Captions,
blur regions, background music and a split vertical layout are in the
editor.

It is free to download and free to record with. Exporting the finished video
needs the $79 licence, which covers three Macs and comes with a 14-day
money-back guarantee. It needs macOS Sonoma 14.0 or later and an Apple
Silicon Mac; Intel Macs are not supported.

## The best Screen Charm alternatives

### How we picked

- **We recorded the same two-minute take in each**, with clicks, typing, a
  camera and system audio, then exported it and looked at the file.
- **Prices come from each vendor's own page** on the date under the table,
  with both billing periods wherever there are two.
- **We checked what the trial lets you do**, because that is the complaint
  at the top of this post and it is the one a feature list hides.
- **We left out** Windows-only apps, anything with no camera track, and
  anything that has not shipped a release in a year.
- **We ranked on the three reasons above**: what you can get out before you
  pay, what the licence costs, and how much of the edit is done for you.

### 1. Prequel

![Prequel's website](/blog/tools/prequel.webp)

Prequel is a Mac screen recorder built around the finished video. You
record, you stop, and the editor opens with the first pass made: zooms
placed on every click and every burst of typing, the camera framed and
shaped in a corner, a background applied with padding and a shadow, the two
audio tracks balanced, and the cursor smoothed, resized and hidden when it
idles.

The zooms carry a perspective tilt and focus falls away from whatever they
aim at. The camera is a separate recording, so you move it, reshape it and
resize it after the take. Captions are transcribed on your Mac with nothing
uploaded. Export is one MP4 at up to 4K and 120 fps, in H.264 or HEVC,
rendered on your own machine.

**Pros**

- The trial is the whole app for seven days, export included, with no watermark
- $29 once, or $9 a month, and both plans have every feature
- 4K at up to 120 fps, where most of this category stops at 60
- Zooms follow your typing as well as your clicks
- The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/haxzie/prequel) under the Functional Source License, which becomes Apache 2.0 two years after each release

**Requires**

- An Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 14 or later
- The Screen Recording permission, granted once

**Our take.** If what stopped you was finishing a video and finding the
export locked, start here: seven days with nothing held back, and the files
you make in that week are yours whether or not you pay afterwards. The $79
that buys Screen Charm buys Prequel nearly three times over, and the zooms
follow what you type, not only where you click.

**Price.** $29 once, or $9 a month. Seven days free with the whole app in
it.

### 2. Screen Studio

![Screen Studio's website](/blog/tools/screen-studio.webp)

Screen Studio started this category in November 2022, and every automatic
zoom recorder since has been measured against it. It smooths the cursor,
zooms on clicks and typing, puts the window on a background, and records an
iPhone or iPad over a USB cable with the right device frame drawn round it.

**Pros**

- Automatic zoom on clicks and on typing, with three years of polish on it
- Records an iPhone or iPad over USB and detects the model and colour
- Reframes a 16:9 take to vertical without cropping it
- Captions transcribed on the Mac; microphone and system audio on separate tracks

**Cons**

- Subscription only since October 2025, with no one-time licence for new customers
- No free tier, and export is behind the paid month
- Export stops at 4K 60; there is no 120 fps option
- macOS only, and the shared link is capped at 30 minutes with Screen Studio's branding on the page

**Our take.** If you want the original and the monthly bill is fine, this is
it. If a one-off licence was the whole reason you looked at Screen Charm,
Screen Studio is the wrong direction, and Prequel's $29 is the thing Screen
Studio withdrew. Prequel also exports at 4K 120 where this stops at 60.

**Price.** $29 a month billed monthly, or $9 a month billed yearly. No free
plan.

### 3. Cap

![Cap's website](/blog/tools/cap.webp)

Cap is the open-source recorder in this field, past 22,000 stars on GitHub
under AGPLv3. Instant mode uploads as you record and hands you a link.
Studio mode records locally and opens a full editor with automatic zooms,
3D scenes, colour correction and camera cutouts. It runs on macOS, Windows
and Linux.

**Pros**

- The code is on GitHub under AGPLv3, and it can be self-hosted with Docker Compose
- macOS 13.1 and later, on Apple Silicon and Intel, plus Windows and Linux
- 4K at 60 fps export on the free tier
- Shares can point at your own S3 bucket or Google Drive on the paid plan

**Cons**

- The free version covers personal use only; using it at work needs a paid plan
- Shareable links stop at five minutes on the free tier and on the $29 licence
- The $29 is per year, and the one-off licence is $58
- No zoom on what you type

**Our take.** If you need Windows or Linux in the same team, or you want to
read the source, Cap is the one on this page. Prequel's source is on GitHub
too, under the Functional Source License, and the difference that matters
day to day is the licence arithmetic: Cap's $29 buys twelve months, Prequel's
$29 buys the app, and there is no separate permission needed to use it for
work.

**Price.** Free for personal use. A Desktop Licence is $29 per year or $58
once, and Cap Pro is $12 per user per month, or $8.16 a month billed
annually.

### 4. CleanShot X

![CleanShot X's website](/blog/tools/cleanshot-x.webp)

CleanShot X has been the Mac's screenshot tool since 2018, and version 5
added a Studio Mode in September 2026 that edits a recording with automatic
zooms, cursor smoothing and backgrounds. If you already own it for
screenshots, the video side is now included.

**Pros**

- The best screenshot and annotation tool on the Mac, by a distance
- Scrolling capture, an overlay annotator, and cloud links in one app
- $35 once, which is less than Screen Charm asks

**Cons**

- The $35 buys the app and one year of updates; after that it is $19 a year to keep updating
- The video editor shipped three weeks ago
- macOS 13 or later, no free tier

**Our take.** Buy it for screenshots, where nothing else comes close. For
demos and walkthroughs, the video half is a month old and Prequel has been
built for nothing else since version one, with the perspective tilt and the
focus falloff that neither of these has.

**Price.** $35 once, including a year of updates, with optional renewal at
$19 a year. CleanShot Pro is $10 per user a month, billed annually.

### 5. OBS Studio

![OBS Studio's website](/blog/tools/obs.webp)

OBS records anything on your Mac, at any resolution, for nothing, with no
account and no cap. It is also a live-streaming desk, which is what most of
its interface is for. It is the only tool on this page that runs on an Intel
Mac and on macOS 12.

**Pros**

- Free and open source under GPL-2.0
- Records several displays, windows, cameras and capture cards at once
- Runs on macOS 12 or newer, on Intel and Apple Silicon, plus Windows and Linux

**Cons**

- No editing at all; the export is the recording
- No automatic zoom, no cursor smoothing, no camera framing
- Setting up scenes and sources takes an afternoon

**Our take.** Stream with it, or use it when your Mac is too old for
anything else here. For a demo somebody outside the company will watch, OBS
gives you raw footage and a second job in an editor, and Prequel gives you
the video.

**Price.** Free.

### 6. QuickTime Player

![QuickTime Player's support page](/blog/tools/quicktime.webp)

QuickTime is on every Mac. File, New Screen Recording, and you have the
screen and the microphone. It is the baseline the rest of this page is
measured against.

**Pros**

- Already installed, free, and reliable
- Records the screen or the camera at full resolution
- No account, no licence, no update to buy

**Cons**

- No system audio on macOS 26 or earlier without a virtual driver
- Screen or camera, never both in one file
- No editing beyond a trim

**Our take.** Use it when you need a capture in the next ten seconds and
nobody outside the room will see it. For anything you send out, Prequel
records the screen, the camera, the microphone and the Mac's own sound as
four separate sources in one take, and hands them back edited.

**Price.** Free with macOS.

## Which one should you switch to?

**If the locked export was the problem**, Prequel. Seven days with the whole
app in it, export included, and the files are yours afterwards either way.

**If you want the original automatic zoom**, Screen Studio or Prequel.
Screen Studio if the monthly bill is fine and you need to record an iPhone,
which nothing else here does; Prequel if you would rather pay once and
export at up to 4K 120 against Screen Studio's 4K 60.

**If you need Windows or Linux, or you want the source**, Cap.

**If you mostly take screenshots**, CleanShot X. **If your Mac is Intel**,
OBS. **If nobody outside the room will see it**, QuickTime.

What Prequel does, since it is the recommendation: it records a display, a
window or an area you drag out, with the camera, the microphone and system
audio each on their own track. When you stop, the editor opens with the
first pass made. Zooms are placed on every click and every burst of typing.
The camera is framed, shaped and sized in a corner. A background is applied
with padding, a radius, a border and a shadow. The two audio tracks are
balanced on separate gains. The cursor is smoothed, resized and hidden when
it idles.

The timeline is where you cut, with waveforms under the clips so you can see
where. Nothing is cut for you. Captions are transcribed on your Mac when you
stop, and you correct a word by typing over it, with nothing sent anywhere.

Export is one MP4 at up to 4K and 120 frames a second, in H.264 or HEVC,
encoded by your Mac's own media engine. There is no upload step, no queue,
no watermark on any plan or during the trial, and no cap on the length of a
take. Every frame preset is there, including the vertical and square ones.

It is $29 once, or $9 a month, with seven days free and the whole app in the
trial. The source is on [GitHub](https://github.com/haxzie/prequel) under
the Functional Source License, which turns into Apache 2.0 two years after
each release. It needs an Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 14 or later and the
Screen Recording permission.
