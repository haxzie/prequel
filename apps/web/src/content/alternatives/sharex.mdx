## What is ShareX, and what is it good at?

ShareX is a free, open-source capture tool for Windows that does more than most
paid ones. Region capture, scrolling capture, OCR, a colour picker, screen
recording to MP4 or GIF, and then an upload pipeline that will send the result
to dozens of destinations and put the link on your clipboard, all driven by
hotkeys and workflows you define yourself.

Nobody pays for it, nothing is locked, and for a Windows user who wants to press
one key and have a link, it is unbeatable.

## Why do people look for a ShareX alternative?

**It does not run on macOS.** ShareX is Windows only, and there is no port. For
anyone switching to a Mac, that is the whole reason this page exists.

**The video side is capture, not production.** ShareX records what is on screen.
There is no zoom on a click, no cursor smoothing, no camera layer, no background
and no timeline. A finished demo means an editor afterwards.

## Is Prequel a good ShareX alternative?

For the video half, on a Mac, yes.

Prequel records the screen, the camera, the microphone and system audio as
separate tracks, then opens the editor with the first pass already made: zooms
placed on every click and every burst of typing, tilted, with focus falling away
from whatever they are aimed at, the camera framed and cut out of its
background, a wallpaper behind the window with padding and a shadow, the cursor
smoothed, blurred as it moves and hidden when it idles. Captions are transcribed
on your Mac.

Export is one file at up to 4K and 120 frames a second, and a shareable link is
one click when you want one. It is $9 a month or $39 once.

## What do you give up by switching?

Free and open source, screenshots, OCR, the colour picker and the upload
pipeline with its dozens of destinations and its custom workflows. Nothing on a
Mac replaces that part of ShareX, and nothing in Prequel tries to.

On macOS, the nearest things to the capture half are CleanShot X and the
built-in Screenshot app; Prequel is the video half.
