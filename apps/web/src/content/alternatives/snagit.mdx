## What is Snagit, and what is it good at?

Snagit is TechSmith's capture tool, and it has been on work laptops for twenty
years. Grab a region, a window, a scrolling page or a whole panoramic shot,
then mark it up: arrows, callouts, numbered steps, blur over anything private,
a cut-out of the bit that matters. It turns a sequence of captures into a
step-by-step guide, and it runs on Windows and Mac, which is why it is the one
tool a mixed team can standardise on.

It records video too, with a webcam and a microphone, and it will turn a short
recording into an animated GIF. Licences are $39 per user per year.

## Why do people look for a Snagit alternative?

**It is a screenshot tool that also records.** The video side is capture, trim
and share. There is no automatic zoom, no cursor smoothing, no background behind
the window, no camera framing. For documentation that is exactly right. For a
product demo it means the finished video is the raw take.

**It is a subscription now.** Snagit was bought once for years, and the current
licence is $39 per user per year. For a tool opened twice a month, a renewal
notice invites a look at what else is out there.

**Camtasia is the upsell.** TechSmith's answer to "I need to edit this
properly" is a second, dearer product, and learning it is a real investment.

## Is Prequel a good Snagit alternative?

For video, yes. For screenshots and step-by-step guides, no.

Prequel records the screen, the camera, the microphone and system audio as
separate tracks and opens the editor with the first pass made: zooms placed on
every click and every burst of typing, tilted, with focus falling away from the
subject, the camera framed over a background, the cursor smoothed and hidden
when it idles. Captions are transcribed on your Mac. The timeline cuts, splits,
retimes and blurs.

Export is one MP4 or HEVC file at up to 4K and 120 frames a second, for $9 a
month or $29 once, with no renewal to approve.

## What do you give up by switching?

Screenshots, and the documentation workflow built on them: numbered steps,
callouts, templates, scrolling capture, the whole image editor. Prequel does not
take a screenshot.

Also Windows. Snagit is the same product on both platforms and Prequel is an
Apple Silicon Mac app, so a mixed team recording how-to guides keeps Snagit.
