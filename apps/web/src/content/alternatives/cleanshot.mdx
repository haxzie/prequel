## What is CleanShot X, and what is it good at?

CleanShot X is the Mac capture toolkit most people buy for screenshots. Hide
your desktop icons before a shot, annotate it, redact a password, drop it on a
background with padding and a shadow, scroll-capture a page taller than the
screen, pull the text out of an image, and keep every capture in a history you
can reach again. There are over fifty features and the screenshot half of it has
no real rival.

It records too: a window or a region, the camera in a bubble, the microphone and
system audio, keystrokes drawn on screen, automatic click highlights, smart
zooms, and an MP4 or a GIF at the end. Studio Mode adds a Mac-native editor for
trimming and zooms. It is $35 once, which includes a year of updates.

## Why do people look for a CleanShot X alternative?

Usually because the recording outgrew the tool.

**The video is the smaller half of the app.** Recording sits alongside
screenshots, annotation, OCR, scrolling capture and the cloud, and it is priced
and built as one feature among many. A product demo that needs a framed camera,
a background behind a window, a cursor that behaves and a timeline with real
cuts is asking for a video editor.

**The licence stops updating after a year.** $35 buys the app and twelve months
of updates. Keeping up afterwards is $19 a year, or $10 a user a month on the
Pro plan with the cloud attached.

## Is Prequel a good CleanShot X alternative?

For the recording, yes. For screenshots, nothing here replaces it.

Prequel records the screen, the camera, the microphone and system audio as
separate tracks and opens the editor with the first pass made: zooms placed on
every click and every burst of typing, tilted, with focus falling away from
whatever the push-in is aimed at. The camera is framed, shaped and cut out of
the room behind you. A background sits behind the window with padding, a radius
and a shadow. Captions are transcribed on your Mac. The timeline cuts, splits
and retimes.

Export is 4K at up to 120 frames a second, and the price is $9 a month or $29
once, with every update included for as long as you hold the licence.

## What do you give up by switching?

Screenshots, and everything CleanShot built round them: annotation, redaction,
scrolling capture, text recognition, pinned shots, the capture history, the
desktop-icon trick. Prequel does not take a screenshot at all, and most people
who switch keep CleanShot for exactly that.

Both are macOS-only, so there is nothing to weigh on platform.
