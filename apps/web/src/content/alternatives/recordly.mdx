## What is Recordly, and what is it good at?

Recordly is a free, open-source screen recorder with the cinematic pass built
in. Auto-zoom on your clicks, an animated cursor with motion blur, a background
behind the recording, a webcam bubble that grows when you need it, a timeline
for speed, zooms, annotations and audio tracks, and an export to MP4 or a
looping GIF. It runs on macOS, Windows and Linux.

It costs nothing, the source is public, and there is no watermark and no paywall
anywhere in it. For a side project, a student, or anyone who wants to read the
code that touches their screen, that is a complete answer.

## Why do people look for a Recordly alternative?

**It is a young project with a small team.** Releases come when they come, and
the surface it covers is the core of the idea rather than the whole of it.

**Free software and finished output are different goals.** The zooms and the
cursor are there. The perspective tilt, the focus falling away from the subject,
zooms on what you type, a camera cut out of the room behind you, captions
transcribed on device and seventeen colour looks are not.

**Nothing to call when it breaks.** That is the deal with free software, and it
is a real cost on the morning a launch video will not export.

## Is Prequel a good Recordly alternative?

If the video is going in front of customers, yes.

Prequel records the screen, the camera, the microphone and system audio as
separate tracks on ScreenCaptureKit, and opens the editor with the pass already
made: zooms on every click and every burst of typing, tilted, with focus falling
away from whatever the push-in is aimed at. The camera is framed, shaped and cut
out of its background by a model that runs on your Mac. Apple's sound isolation
cleans the microphone. Captions are transcribed on device.

Export is one MP4 or HEVC file at up to 4K and 120 frames a second, encoded by
the Mac's own media engine, at $9 a month or $29 once.

## What do you give up by switching?

Free, open source, Windows and Linux. Recordly costs nothing and runs on three
platforms; Prequel is a paid Mac app on Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later.

If free is the hard requirement, Recordly is a good answer and so is OpenScreen,
and this page is not trying to talk you out of either.
