## What is Movavi Screen Recorder, and what is it good at?

Movavi Screen Recorder is a capture utility for Windows and Mac, from a company
that sells a suite of them. It records a full screen or a region with the
webcam, the microphone and system audio, draws on the picture while you record,
shows keystrokes and mouse clicks, schedules a recording for a webinar you will
be asleep for, takes scrolling screenshots and reduces microphone noise. Capture
goes up to 4K and there is a light editor for trimming and joining.

It is $44.95 a year or $74.95 once, with a free trial that watermarks the
export.

## Why do people look for a Movavi alternative?

**The upsell is the product.** Screen Recorder trims and joins. Anything beyond
that points at Video Editor, Video Converter or the bundle, each with its own
price, and the free trial puts a watermark on the file so the first real export
is after the purchase.

**Nothing is automatic.** Clicks are drawn as rings, which is useful, but there
is no zoom placed where you clicked, no cursor smoothing, no background behind
the window and no camera framing. The recording is the take.

## Is Prequel a good Movavi alternative?

On a Mac, yes, and for less.

Prequel records the screen, the camera, the microphone and system audio as
separate tracks and opens the editor with the pass already made: zooms on every
click and every burst of typing, tilted, with focus falling away from the
subject, the camera framed, shaped and cut out of the room behind you, a
background with padding and a shadow, the cursor smoothed and hidden when it
idles. Apple's own sound isolation cleans the microphone. Captions are
transcribed on your Mac.

Export is one MP4 or HEVC file at up to 4K and 120 frames a second, for $9 a
month or $29 once, and the seven-day trial exports without a watermark.

## What do you give up by switching?

Windows, and the scheduler. Movavi is the same product on both platforms and
will start a recording at a time you set, which is genuinely useful for
webinars. Prequel records when you press record, on an Apple Silicon Mac
running macOS 14 or later.

Also drawing on the screen while recording, which Movavi does and Prequel does
not.
