## What is Clipchamp, and what is it good at?

Clipchamp is Microsoft's video editor, and on Windows 11 it is already
installed. Record your screen, your camera and your voice, cut on a timeline,
add text, transitions, filters and stock footage, then export at 1080p with no
watermark on the free plan. It runs as a Windows app or in Chrome and Edge, and
it is tied into Microsoft 365: Editor Standard is $3 a user a month paid yearly,
Premium is $7 and brings 4K.

For somebody on a work PC who needs to trim a recording and put a title on it,
nothing is cheaper or closer to hand.

## Why do people look for a Clipchamp alternative?

**There is no Mac app.** Clipchamp is a Windows app or a browser tab. On a Mac
it runs in Chrome or Edge, with everything that implies for recording the
machine it is running on.

**4K is behind Premium.** The free plan and Standard both stop at 1080p.

**Editing is manual, which is what an editor means.** Zooms are keyframes you
set yourself, the camera is a layer you place, and there is nothing that reads
where you clicked. A demo that looks directed is an afternoon of work.

## Is Prequel a good Clipchamp alternative?

On a Mac, yes, and it starts further along.

Prequel records the screen, the camera, the microphone and system audio as
separate tracks and opens the editor with the first pass already made: zooms
placed on every click and every burst of typing, tilted, with focus falling away
from whatever the push-in is aimed at, the camera framed and cut out of its
background, a wallpaper behind the window with padding and a shadow, the cursor
smoothed. Captions are transcribed on your Mac.

Export is one file at up to 4K and 120 frames a second, encoded by the Mac's own
media engine, at $9 a month or $39 once.

## What do you give up by switching?

Windows, the Microsoft 365 account it bills through, and a general-purpose
editor. Clipchamp cuts footage from any source, with stock music and video in
the library. Prequel edits a recording it made, or a video you import into a
project, and it carries no stock library.

Also free at 1080p, which Clipchamp genuinely is.
