## What is Cursorful, and what is it good at?

Cursorful started as a Chrome extension that recorded a tab and zoomed it
automatically, and it now has Mac and Windows apps as well. Zooms follow your
clicks, the webcam docks in a corner in a circle or a rounded rectangle, the
browser frame can be hidden with one click, captions are generated on your own
machine, and rendering happens locally up to 4K.

Pro is $79 once per seat, listed at $119, and the free tier covers
non-commercial use. For recording a web app in the browser you already have it
open in, the extension is genuinely the shortest path there is.

## Why do people look for a Cursorful alternative?

**Free is non-commercial.** Recording a demo for your own product is commercial
use, so the licence is the price of the first real video.

**The browser is the home ground.** The desktop apps exist, and the product's
shape, its crop-to-the-recorded-window, its hide-the-tab-strip, its extension
install, is still built around recording a tab.

**Zooms are triggered by a rule you do not set.** Two or more clicks inside a
three-second window is the documented trigger. Typing is not a trigger at all.

## Is Prequel a good Cursorful alternative?

For a Mac recording of anything, in or out of a browser, yes.

Prequel captures a display, a window or a region through ScreenCaptureKit, with
the camera, the microphone and system audio on separate tracks, and opens the
editor with the pass made: zooms placed on every click and every burst of
typing, tilted, with focus falling away from whatever the push-in is aimed at.
The camera is framed, shaped and cut out of its background. A wallpaper sits
behind the window with padding, a radius and a shadow. Captions are transcribed
on your Mac.

Export is one file at up to 4K and 120 frames a second, for $9 a month or $29
once against their $79.

## What do you give up by switching?

The browser extension, and with it the Chrome, Edge and Brave install on Windows
and Linux. If what you record is always a tab and you want it recorded from
inside the browser, Cursorful is built for exactly that and Prequel is not.

Cursorful's Pro licence is also a single payment with no subscription option,
which some people prefer on principle.
