## What is ScreenPal, and what is it good at?

ScreenPal is Screencast-O-Matic, renamed. It records your screen on Windows,
Mac, Chromebook, Android and iOS, edits in the browser or in the app, hosts the
result, and adds quizzing, analytics and captions on top. The free plan records
for fifteen minutes with no watermark on the file, which is unusually generous,
and paid plans start at $4 a month billed annually.

Teachers and course creators are the audience it was built for and still serves
best: a lesson, a quiz inside the video, a link for a class, and a price that a
department can sign off without a meeting.

## Why do people look for a ScreenPal alternative?

**The video is edited, not directed.** The editor is a full one, with
transitions, overlays, captions and multiple audio tracks, and all of it is
manual. Nothing places a zoom where you clicked, smooths the cursor, frames a
camera over a background or pulls focus. A polished walkthrough is an hour of
arranging clips.

**It spans five platforms, so it is native to none.** The compromise shows up in
the editor, which is a web application doing a desktop job, and in export, where
the heavy work happens somewhere other than your own GPU.

## Is Prequel a good ScreenPal alternative?

For a Mac recording that needs to look produced, yes.

Prequel records the screen, the camera, the microphone and system audio as
separate tracks and opens the editor with the pass already made: zooms on every
click and every burst of typing, tilted, with focus falling away from whatever
the push-in is aimed at. The camera is framed, shaped and cut out of the room.
A background goes behind the window with padding and a shadow. The cursor is
smoothed. Captions are transcribed on your Mac with nothing uploaded.

Export is one file at up to 4K and 120 frames a second, encoded by the Mac's own
media engine, at $9 a month or $39 once.

## What do you give up by switching?

Hosting and teaching. ScreenPal keeps your videos, puts quizzes inside them,
reports who watched what, and gives a class a link. Prequel exports a file and
can share a link, and none of the quizzing or the analytics comes with it.

Also every platform but macOS. ScreenPal records on a Chromebook, a phone and a
PC; Prequel needs an Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 14 or later.
