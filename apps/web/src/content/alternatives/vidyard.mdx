## What is Vidyard, and what is it good at?

Vidyard is video for sales teams. Record a screen or a camera message, send it
in an email or a sequence, and watch what happens next: who opened it, how far
they watched, which part they replayed, and all of it written back into
Salesforce or HubSpot. There are AI avatars, AI-generated prospecting videos,
landing pages, calls to action and a hosting platform underneath.

The free plan records five videos a month, thirty minutes each, with no hosting.
Starter is $59 a seat a month billed annually, and teams go up from there.

## Why do people look for a Vidyard alternative?

**The price is a sales tool's price.** $59 a seat a month is reasonable for a
rep whose pipeline it feeds, and a lot of money for a founder recording a
product demo twice a week. The free plan's five videos a month make that
decision quickly.

**The recorder is the small part.** Vidyard's value is the analytics, the CRM
integrations and the sequences. The recording itself is a face in a bubble over
a screen, and nothing about it is directed: no zooms, no framing, no background,
no cursor work.

## Is Prequel a good Vidyard alternative?

For making the video, yes. For the pipeline attached to it, no.

Prequel records the screen, the camera, the microphone and system audio as
separate tracks and opens the editor with the pass already made: zooms on every
click and every burst of typing, tilted, with focus falling away from whatever
the push-in is aimed at, the camera framed and cut out of the room, a background
behind the window, the cursor smoothed. Captions are transcribed on your Mac.

Export is one file at up to 4K and 120 frames a second, and the price is $9 a
month or $39 once, against $59 a seat.

## What do you give up by switching?

The entire sales layer: viewer analytics, CRM write-back, email sequences,
landing pages, calls to action, AI avatars and prospecting videos. If a rep is
measured on reply rates, Vidyard is doing a job Prequel does not attempt.

Also Windows and the browser extension. Prequel is macOS on Apple Silicon.
