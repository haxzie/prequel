## What is Cap, and what is it good at?

Cap is the open-source screen recorder people reach for instead of Loom. Record
locally in Studio Mode with a full editor, or record and share a link in
seconds. It runs on macOS and Windows, the source is public, and it can be
self-hosted: a team that cannot send video to someone else's server can run the
whole thing on its own infrastructure, with custom S3 or Google Drive storage
behind it.

It is free for personal use with no time limit on local recordings. Commercial
use is a Desktop Licence at $29 a year or $58 once. Cap Pro, at $12 a user a
month or $8.16 billed annually, is where the cloud lives: unlimited sharing, a
custom domain, password-protected links, viewer analytics, AI titles, summaries,
chapters and transcripts.

## Why do people look for a Cap alternative?

Two reasons, and they are different reasons.

**Free is for personal use.** Recording for work needs a paid licence, and the
free plan caps a shareable link at five minutes. That is a fair line, drawn
clearly, and it still surprises people who tried Cap on a demo for their own
product.

**The output is a clean recording, not a directed one.** Cap exports 4K at 60,
and the editor gives you zooms and the usual trims. What it does not do is put a
tilt on the push-in, pull focus away from the subject, place a zoom on what you
type, or cut your camera out of the room behind you.

## Is Prequel a good Cap alternative?

For the finished video, yes.

Prequel records the screen, the camera, the microphone and system audio as
separate tracks, then opens the editor with the pass already made. Zooms land on
every click and every burst of typing, tilted, with focus falling away from
whatever they are aimed at. The camera is framed, shaped and cut out of its
background by a model running on your Mac. Seventeen colour looks sit over the
picture. Captions are transcribed on device.

Export is one file at up to 4K and 120 frames a second against Cap's 4K 60, and
the licence is $9 a month or $29 once, which covers commercial work from the
first recording.

## What do you give up by switching?

The open-source licence and the ability to self-host, which for some teams is
the only reason Cap is on the list. Also Windows, the Loom importer, the custom
domain, the viewer analytics and the AI chapters.

Cap's one-off licence is $58 against Prequel's $29, so the one-time route is
cheaper here, and the cloud half is not the same product. If link analytics are
what you are buying, Cap Pro keeps that argument.
