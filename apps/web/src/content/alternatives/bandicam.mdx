## What is Bandicam, and what is it good at?

Bandicam is a Windows recorder built for long captures and for games. It will
record for twenty-four hours without falling over, hooks DirectX and OpenGL to
capture a game at high frame rates with hardware encoding, records a device over
HDMI, and draws on the screen while you record. It is the tool of choice for
anyone capturing hours of gameplay or a full day of a running machine.

A personal licence is $33.26 a year or $53.90 for a perpetual one. The free
version adds a watermark and stops a recording after ten minutes.

## Why do people look for a Bandicam alternative?

Most often because they moved to a Mac.

**There is no Mac version.** Bandicam is Windows software. No build exists for
macOS, so the question is not which settings to carry over but what to use
instead.

**It captures rather than directs.** Even on Windows the output is the take:
no zoom placed on a click, no cursor smoothing, no background behind the window,
no camera framing. Editing means Bandicut or another editor afterwards.

## Is Prequel a good Bandicam alternative?

On a Mac, for demos and tutorials, yes.

Prequel records a display, a window or a region with the camera, the microphone
and system audio as separate tracks, and opens the editor with the first pass
made: zooms on every click and every burst of typing, tilted, with focus falling
away from whatever the push-in is aimed at, the camera framed over a background,
the cursor smoothed. Captions are transcribed on your Mac. The timeline cuts,
splits, retimes and blurs.

Export is one MP4 or HEVC file at up to 4K and 120 frames a second through the
Mac's own media engine, at $9 a month or $39 once, with no watermark at any
point including the seven-day trial.

## What do you give up by switching?

Game capture, and Windows with it. Prequel records what the window server draws,
not a DirectX surface, and it has no HDMI device capture and no twenty-four-hour
session. For gameplay on a PC, Bandicam stays the better tool and OBS is the
free one.

On a Mac, game capture is ScreenCaptureKit either way, and Prequel needs Apple
Silicon and macOS 14 or later.
