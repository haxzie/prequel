## What is Screencastify, and what is it good at?

Screencastify is a screen recorder that lives in Chrome. Install the extension,
record a tab, your desktop or just the camera, and the video goes to Google
Drive or to Screencastify's own library with a link ready to share. Editing is
trim and stitch, text and blur boxes, and editing by transcript. Questions and
polls can be dropped into a video for a class to answer.

It is free for up to ten videos with a thirty-minute cap on each, then $7 a user
a month billed annually, or $19 monthly. Classrooms on Chromebooks are what it
was built for, and in that setting it is hard to beat: nothing to install beyond
an extension, and everything lands in Drive.

## Why do people look for a Screencastify alternative?

**It is a browser extension, with a browser's limits.** Recording quality, frame
rate and what the machine can do while recording are all bounded by the tab it
runs in. There is no system audio in the way a native recorder has it, and the
camera is a bubble on top rather than a layer you can reframe afterwards.

**The free plan runs out at ten videos.** Not ten a month. Ten.

**Nothing is automatic.** No zoom on a click, no cursor smoothing, no
background, no framing. The finished video is the take, trimmed.

## Is Prequel a good Screencastify alternative?

If you are on a Mac and the video has to look produced, yes.

Prequel records the screen, the camera, the microphone and system audio as
separate tracks, with no extension and no browser involved, then opens the
editor with the first pass made: zooms on every click and every burst of typing,
tilted, with focus falling away from the subject, the camera framed over a
background, the cursor smoothed and blurred as it moves. Captions are
transcribed on your Mac.

Export is one file at up to 4K and 120 frames a second, at $9 a month or $29
once.

## What do you give up by switching?

Chromebooks, Google Drive and the classroom. Screencastify is an extension a
school can push to every device, with videos in Drive and quizzes inside them.
Prequel is a Mac app with a Screen Recording permission, and it has no quizzing
and no Drive integration.

Also Windows, since Screencastify runs wherever Chrome does.
