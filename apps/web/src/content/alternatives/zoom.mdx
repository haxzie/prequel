## What is Zoom, and what is it good at?

Zoom is the meeting. It is also, for a lot of people, the screen recorder they
already have: start a meeting with nobody in it, share your screen, press
record, and you have an MP4 with your face in the corner and your voice over it.
No install, no new tool, no explaining to anyone why there is another app on the
expense form.

Basic is free with a forty-minute cap on group meetings. Pro is $14.16 a user a
month billed annually and adds local and 10 GB of cloud recording, and Business
is $18.33.

## Why do people look for a Zoom alternative?

Because a recorded meeting looks like a recorded meeting.

**The output is a call, not a demo.** One fixed distance for the whole take, the
camera in a rectangle in the corner, the compression a video call uses, and a
cursor that is wherever your hand left it. Nothing zooms, nothing is framed,
nothing is cut.

**The recorder is a side effect.** Settings that matter for a demo, like frame
rate and what the camera layer is allowed to do, are meeting settings, and some
of them are controlled by whoever administers the account rather than by you.

## Is Prequel a good Zoom alternative?

For recording a demo, yes. For meeting anyone, no.

Prequel records the screen, the camera, the microphone and system audio as
separate tracks, which is the difference the finished file shows: the camera can
be reframed, reshaped and cut out of the room afterwards, and the audio can be
balanced track by track. The editor opens with zooms already placed on every
click and every burst of typing, tilted, with focus falling away from the
subject, a background behind the window, and the cursor smoothed.

Export is one MP4 or HEVC file at up to 4K and 120 frames a second, at $9 a
month or $39 once.

## What do you give up by switching?

Other people. Zoom is a meeting platform with a recorder attached, and Prequel
has no meetings, no participants, no screen sharing and no phone system. Keeping
Zoom is not the same decision as keeping a screen recorder.

Zoom also runs everywhere. Prequel needs an Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 14 or
later.
