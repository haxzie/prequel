## What is Icecream Screen Recorder, and what is it good at?

Icecream Screen Recorder is a small, cheap capture utility for Windows and Mac.
Pick an area or a window, record it with the microphone, the webcam and system
audio, draw on the screen while you record, take a screenshot from the same
toolbar, and trim the result afterwards. It is the kind of tool that does the
job in two clicks and asks nothing of you.

PRO is $29.95 a year or $59.95 for a lifetime licence on Windows. The free
version is where most people meet it, and it is the free version's limits that
send them looking.

## Why do people look for an Icecream alternative?

**The free version watermarks and caps.** The upgrade page is explicit about
what PRO removes: the watermark and the limit on how long a recording can run.
That is a reasonable way to sell a utility, and it is also the moment people
start comparing.

**It is a capture tool, not a video tool.** Trimming is the edit. There is no
zoom placed where you clicked, no cursor smoothing, no background, no camera
framing, no captions. The file is the take.

## Is Prequel a good Icecream alternative?

On a Mac, yes.

Prequel records the screen, the camera, the microphone and system audio as
separate tracks and opens the editor with the first pass made: zooms on every
click and every burst of typing, tilted, with focus falling away from the
subject, the camera framed and cut out of its background, a wallpaper behind the
window with padding and a shadow, the cursor smoothed. Captions are transcribed
on your Mac with nothing uploaded.

Export is one MP4 or HEVC file at up to 4K and 120 frames a second, and nothing
is ever watermarked, including in the seven-day trial. It is $9 a month or $29
once.

## What do you give up by switching?

Windows, and drawing on the screen while you record. Icecream is the same small
tool on both platforms, and Prequel needs an Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 14 or
later.

Also screenshots from the same toolbar, which Icecream has and Prequel does not.
