## What is Screen Charm, and what is it good at?

Screen Charm is a one-person Mac screen recorder that edits the take for you.
Record a display, a window or an area with the camera, the microphone and the
Mac's own sound, and every click gets a zoom with motion blur on it, the cursor
is smoothed, and the window sits on a gradient, a photo or inside a MacBook
frame. Captions, blur regions, a teleprompter, background music and a split
vertical layout for Shorts are in the editor.

Sergey Nazarov has shipped it since July 2025, usually a release every couple of
weeks, and it reached #4 Product of the Day on Product Hunt in June 2026. It
costs $99, once, for up to three Macs, and there is no subscription.

If one payment is the whole requirement and the MacBook frame or the music
library is the look you want, Screen Charm does that well.

## Why do people look for a Screen Charm alternative?

Two things, mostly.

**You can record and edit for free, and then you cannot export.** The app is
free to download and record with. The finished video stays inside it until you
have paid the $99, so the first time you see your own export is after the
purchase. There is a 14-day refund if it is not what you hoped, but that is a
refund, not a trial.

**$99 is the dearest one-off licence in this category.** The field under it is
busy: Cap is $58 once, CleanShot X is $35, Prequel is $39, and there is a new
auto-zoom Mac recorder on Show HN every few weeks, most of them under $30. Screen
Charm's own answer to that is the frequency of its releases, which is fair, and
the price went up from $79 to $99 in the autumn of 2026 rather than down.

## Is Prequel a good Screen Charm alternative?

For the video, yes, and for the same kind of licence at a third of the money.

Prequel records the screen, the camera, the microphone and system audio as
separate tracks and opens the editor with the first pass made: zooms placed on
every click _and every burst of typing_, tilted, with focus falling away from
whatever the push-in is aimed at. The camera is framed and shaped afterwards. A
background is applied with padding and a shadow. The cursor is smoothed, blurred
as it moves and hidden when it idles. Captions are transcribed on your Mac with
nothing uploaded.

Export is one MP4 or HEVC file at up to 4K and 120 frames a second, encoded by
the Mac's own media engine, against Screen Charm's 4K 60. The download is 104 MB
against their 237.

On price it is $39 once, which is the one-off licence Screen Charm sells for
$99, or $9 a month if you would rather not commit. The seven-day trial is the
whole app, export included, so the files you make in that week are yours whether
or not you pay afterwards.

## What do you give up by switching?

The MacBook device frame and the built-in music library. Screen Charm wraps a
recording in a laptop and lets you pick a track from a bundled set; Prequel puts
a frame of padding, a radius, a border and a shadow round the recording and
leaves the music to you.

Both need an Apple Silicon Mac on macOS 14 or later, so there is nothing to
weigh there.
