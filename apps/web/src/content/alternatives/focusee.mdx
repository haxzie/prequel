## What is FocuSee, and what is it good at?

FocuSee records your screen and zooms it for you. Clicks become push-ins, the
cursor is smoothed and enlarged, the recording sits on a background with a
radius and a shadow, and the result can be reframed for a vertical feed. It runs
on Windows and Mac, which makes it one of the few auto-zoom recorders a mixed
team can both use, and it comes from iMobie, a company with a catalogue of
consumer apps behind it.

Standard is $49.99 a year or $19.99 a month. Advanced is $79.99 a year, $24.99
a month, or $199.99 once for a lifetime licence to the 2.x line.

## Why do people look for a FocuSee alternative?

**The lifetime licence is $199.99, and it is version-bound.** It covers 2.x.
A major version after that is a separate decision, and $199.99 is the dearest
one-off price in this category by a distance.

**The trial exports one video.** You get a single 4K export to judge it by, and
after that the watermark is the reminder.

**Two platforms, one codebase.** The Mac build is not a Mac app in the way
ScreenCaptureKit and VideoToolbox make one, and it shows in export speed and in
how the app sits on the system.

## Is Prequel a good FocuSee alternative?

For the same job on a Mac, yes, at a fifth of the one-off price.

Prequel records the screen, the camera, the microphone and system audio as
separate tracks and opens the editor with the pass already made: zooms on every
click and every burst of typing, tilted, with focus falling away from whatever
the push-in is aimed at. The camera is framed, shaped and cut out of the room
behind you. Seventeen colour looks sit over the picture. The cursor is smoothed,
blurred as it moves and hidden when it idles. Captions are transcribed on your
Mac.

Export is one MP4 or HEVC file at up to 4K and 120 frames a second, encoded by
the Mac's own media engine. It is $39 once, against $199.99, or $9 a month, and
the seven-day trial exports as many videos as you like without a watermark.

## What do you give up by switching?

Windows. FocuSee is the same app on both platforms and Prequel is macOS on
Apple Silicon, so a team recording on PCs keeps FocuSee.

Also the AI editing features iMobie packages around it, which are a different
product idea from a recorder that finishes the take.
