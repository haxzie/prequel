## What is Tella, and what is it good at?

Tella records polished video and then hosts it for you. Automatic zoom on
clicks, backgrounds, borders, camera reframing, auto layouts, AI audio
enhancement, transcript editing and filler-word removal — and then the finished
video lands on a branded page with a custom domain, password protection, calls
to action and per-viewer analytics.

That last part is the real product. Tella is the only tool on this page that
closes the whole loop from recording to distribution to knowing who watched.
It runs on macOS, Windows, a Chrome extension and in a browser, so it works for
teams that are not all on Macs.

If the link and what happens after it matters as much as the video, Tella is a
strong choice and there is no local recorder that replaces it.

## Why do people look for a Tella alternative?

Two things, mostly.

**There is no free plan.** Tella's own help pages say so plainly: it is a paid
product with no forever-free tier. There is a seven-day trial, and exporting
your recordings requires a subscription. Pro is $13 per user per month, Premium
is $19, and 60 fps export is Premium only. For a team that is a line item; for
one person recording occasionally it is a subscription for something used a few
times a month.

**Everything is in the cloud.** Every recording gets a hosted Tella page. That
is the feature, and it is also the constraint — the video exists on their
infrastructure, and per-seat pricing means paying for people whose only
involvement is watching.

## Is Prequel a good Tella alternative?

For producing the video, yes. For distributing it, no.

Prequel records screen, webcam, microphone and system audio as separate tracks,
places zooms from where you click *and what you type*, frames the camera
afterwards, applies a background, cleans up the cursor and exports one MP4 at up
to 4K and 120 frames per second — against Tella's 4K 60, and with no Premium
tier gating the frame rate.

All of it happens on your Mac. There is no upload, no render queue and no hosted
page, because there is no server involved in making the file.

It is bought once rather than per seat, and the free tier is a tier rather than
a trial — no watermark, limited on length and export format instead.

## What do you give up by switching?

The distribution half, completely. No hosted pages, no custom domain, no
password protection, no calls to action, no viewer analytics, no funnels. If you
are using Tella to run outreach or measure engagement, Prequel replaces none of
that — you would be exporting a file and putting it wherever you already put
files.

Also Windows and the browser recorder. Tella works for a mixed team; Prequel is
macOS on Apple Silicon.
