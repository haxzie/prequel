## The Recording panel

The panel is named for what it dresses: the recorded screen, as opposed to the frame around it.

![The Recording panel: padding, corner radius, border and shadow](/docs/inspector-recording.webp)

- **Padding** is the gap between the recording and the frame's edge, as a share of the frame.
- **Corner radius** rounds the recording's corners.
- **Border**: **Width**, **Colour** and **Opacity** for an edge around the recording. A thin, low-opacity border reads as a hairline and is the usual choice; a solid white edge at full opacity reads as a frame around a frame.
- **Shadow**: **Opacity**, **Blur** and **Offset** for a drop shadow onto the background.

## Full-frame layouts

A layout with the screen at full frame has no padding to show a shadow or a corner in, so these controls do nothing until you choose a padded layout. See [Layout](/docs/layout).
