## Transcription

A recording with a microphone track is transcribed when it opens, on the Mac itself using Apple's speech recognition. Nothing is uploaded. The Captions panel shows the progress: **Getting ready to transcribe…** while a speech model is fetched the first time, then **Transcribing…**, then the captions appear. A take with nothing said in it ends on **No speech to caption**.

![The Captions panel: show captions, style, font, size and placement](/docs/inspector-captions.webp)

**Show captions** draws them in the composition and in the export. It is on by default once a transcript exists.

## Style

Six looks: **Subtitle**, **Blur in**, **Highlight**, **Pop**, **Outline** and **Band**. Each animates the words as they are spoken in its own way.

**Text** sets the **Font** (System, Helvetica Neue, Avenir Next, Futura, Optima, Georgia, Times, Courier or Menlo) and the **Size**.

**Placement** puts the captions at the **Top**, **Middle** or **Bottom**, sets the **Distance from edge** and how many **Lines** show at once. **Spoken word** is the colour that marks the word being said.

## Editing the words

**Edit captions** opens the transcript as text you can type into.

![The caption editor, with the transcript as editable text](/docs/caption-editor.webp)

- Click a word and retype it to correct it. The timing is kept.
- Select a run of words and press **Backspace** to cut that footage from the recording. Deleting a sentence from the transcript means deleting it from the video, and the timeline shows the cut.
- **Command + Z** undoes either.

## Captions on the share page

When you share a recording, the transcript travels with it. The share page on prequel.sh shows chapters drawn from what was said, and offers the captions as subtitles. See [Share links](/docs/share-links).
