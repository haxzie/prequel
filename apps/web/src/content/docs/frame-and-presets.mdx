## The frame bar

The bar above the composition sets the output size. **Automatic** matches the recording's own dimensions, which is what a fresh take opens on. The two fields beside the menu take an exact width and height; typing one marks the frame **Custom**.

## Changing shape after the fact

Everything about the look is stored as a fraction of the frame's shorter edge, not in pixels. A camera at a third of the height stays a third of the height when you switch from 16:9 to 9:16, and padding keeps the same proportion. Change the frame late and the layout follows.

Some layouts need a frame wider than it is tall; those are greyed out in a vertical frame.

## Export size

The frame is the picture's size. **Quality** in the export dialog can scale it down to 1080p, 720p or 480p without changing the frame itself. See [Export](/docs/export).
