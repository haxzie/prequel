## Choosing a layout

The Layout panel offers arrangements in three groups.

![The Layout panel, with Padded screen, camera over selected](/docs/inspector-layout.webp)

**Screen and camera**

- Full screen, camera over
- Padded screen, camera over
- Padded screen, camera standing over its right end
- Padded screen, camera standing over its left end
- Screen and camera side by side, camera at the right
- Screen and camera side by side, camera at the left
- Screen above the camera
- Split down the middle

**Camera only**: full frame, padded, or standing further back.

**Screen only**: full frame, padded, or standing further back.

A fresh recording opens on **Padded screen, camera over**. Layouts that need a landscape frame are unavailable in a vertical one.

## Arranging by hand

Everything in the composition can be moved. Click the screen or the camera to select it, then drag to move it or use the corner handles to resize. Guides snap it to the frame's centre and edges.

- Hold **Option** while dragging to pan the picture inside its own box rather than moving the box.
- Hold **Shift** while resizing to constrain the proportions.
- **Esc** clears the selection.

Once you have dragged something, the layout reads **Custom, dragged by hand**. Choosing a preset again snaps everything back.

## Per clip

Select a clip on the timeline before choosing a layout and it applies to that clip alone, so a take can open on the camera and cut to the screen. See the note on overrides in [The editor](/docs/editor-overview).
