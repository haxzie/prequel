## Three kinds

![The Background panel on the Image tab, showing a catalogue of wallpapers](/docs/inspector-background.webp)

- **Image**: a picture. Prequel ships a catalogue of wallpapers in a few categories, downloaded as you choose them. **My wallpaper** uses your Mac's own desktop picture. **Choose an image…** opens any picture of your own.
- **Solid**: one colour from a grid, or **Custom colour** for any other.
- **Gradient**: two colours and an **Angle**.

**Blur**, shown under Image, softens the picture so the recording in front of it stands out.

## Where the picture goes

A background image is copied into the recording's own folder when you choose it. That keeps a recording self-contained: move the folder to another Mac and it exports with the same picture. See [Where files live](/docs/where-files-live).

## Offline

The catalogue is fetched from prequel.sh. Without a connection the picker shows what has already been downloaded, and one wallpaper always ships inside the app so a fresh recording never opens on an empty background.
