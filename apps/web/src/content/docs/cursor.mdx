## When the panel appears

Prequel records the pointer as its own layer rather than baking it into the frames, so it can be changed afterwards. The Cursor panel appears for those recordings. If **Bake the pointer into the recording** was on in Settings when the take was made, the pointer is already in the picture and there is nothing to restyle.

![The Cursor panel: pointer style, size, smoothing, motion blur and hiding](/docs/inspector-cursor.webp)

## Style and size

- **Pointer** shows or hides it entirely.
- **Style**: Black, White, Classic Black, Classic White, Circle, Point, and a set of playful ones: Hammer, Wrench, Cooking, Baby bottle, Corn, Fish, Frog, Hamster and Football. A style sets the arrow; the hand and the text cursor that go with it are chosen to match.
- **Name tags**: five coloured pointers — blue, purple, pink, orange and green — with a name beside them, the way a collaborator's cursor is drawn in a shared canvas. Pick one and a **Name on the tag** field appears under the styles; what you type is drawn on the tag in the preview and the export. The name is kept if you switch to a plain pointer and back. Leave it empty and the pointer is drawn on its own.
- **Size** scales the pointer, as a share of the frame.

## Motion

- **Smoothing** steadies the pointer's path, so a shaky hand reads as a deliberate move.
- **Motion blur** streaks the pointer during fast moves, as a camera would.

## Hiding

- **Hide while typing** fades the pointer out while keys are being pressed. This needs the recording to know when you typed, which is what the Accessibility permission provides.
- **Hide when still** fades it after it has stopped moving; **After** sets how long to wait.
