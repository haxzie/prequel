## Opening Settings

Right-click the menu bar icon and choose **Settings…**, or click **Settings** in the sidebar of the app window.

![The Settings pane](/docs/settings.webp)

## General

- **Open Prequel at login** adds the app to your login items so the menu bar icon is there after a restart. Only an installed copy, one in Applications, can be opened at login.

## Recording

- **Countdown before recording**: **Off**, **3s**, **5s** or **10s**. See [Shortcuts and countdown](/docs/shortcuts-and-countdown).

## Audio

- **Record system audio** captures the Mac's own sound as a separate track. On by default.

## Cursor

- **Bake the pointer into the recording** draws the pointer into the frames as it appeared, instead of recording it as a layer you can restyle later. Off by default. See [Cursor](/docs/cursor).

## Keyboard

- **Note when keys are pressed** keeps the moment of each press and whether it was a letter, the space bar, Return, Delete or a modifier — never which letter. It is what the editor's typing sounds are made from. On by default. Off keeps only the rough stretches of typing the pointer hides behind. Passwords are never noted either way. See [Audio](/docs/audio).

## Permissions

The same four rows as the welcome window, each with its state and an **Allow** button. See [Permissions](/docs/permissions).

## Shortcuts

- **Start and stop recording**: click the field and press a new chord. It has to include Command, Control or Option. See [Shortcuts and countdown](/docs/shortcuts-and-countdown).

## Version

The foot of the pane shows the version you are on and a **Check for Updates** button, which reads **Downloading…** and then **Restart to update** as an update comes in.
