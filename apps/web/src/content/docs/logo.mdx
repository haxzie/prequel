## Adding a logo

The Logo panel places a picture of your own over the video. Click to choose an image; a PNG with transparency works best.

![The Logo panel: placement map, size and opacity](/docs/inspector-logo.webp)

- **Placement** puts it at one of nine positions in the frame. Drag it in the composition for any other.
- **Size** scales it, as a share of the frame.
- **Opacity** fades it, so it sits over the footage without covering it.

The image is copied into the recording's folder, like a background, so the recording stays self-contained.

## Saving it with a look

A logo is part of a scene preset. Save a look with a logo in place and applying that look elsewhere brings the logo with it. See [Scene presets](/docs/scene-presets).
