## What you need

Prequel runs on Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 14 or newer. There is no Intel build and no Windows build.

## Download and open

1. Download the disk image from [prequel.sh/download](/download).
2. Open it and drag Prequel to your Applications folder.
3. Open Prequel from Applications.

The first launch opens a short welcome window. It walks you through the permissions macOS needs, offers to sign you in so you can share recordings, and shows the shortcut that starts a take. You can skip the sign in and come back to it later.

## Where the app lives

Prequel has no Dock icon and no window until you ask for one. It lives in the menu bar. Click the icon to open the recording dock; right-click it for the menu.

![The recording dock, with Entire screen chosen and a camera and microphone switched on](/docs/dock-setup.webp)

Because there is no Dock icon, the menu bar is also how you quit: right-click the icon and choose **Quit Prequel**.

## Staying up to date

Prequel checks for updates itself and installs them when you relaunch. The dock shows an **Update** pill when one is waiting. You can also check by hand from the menu bar with **Check for Updates…**, or from the foot of the Settings pane.
