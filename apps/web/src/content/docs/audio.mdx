## Two sources

A recording can carry two audio tracks: your **Microphone**, and the Mac's own **System audio**. Each is recorded separately so they can be mixed afterwards.

![The Audio panel: include and volume for the microphone and for system audio](/docs/inspector-audio.webp)

For each source:

- **Include** puts it in the export or leaves it out.
- **Volume** sets its level.

The panel is only offered for recordings that have at least one audio track, and it says so when there is none.

## Recording system audio

Whether the Mac's sound is captured at all is a recording-time setting: **Record system audio** in Settings, on by default. A take made with it off has no system track to include later.

## GIF

A GIF export is silent whatever is set here. The export dialog says so in its summary line.
