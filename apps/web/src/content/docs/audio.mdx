## Two sources

A recording can carry two audio tracks: your **Microphone**, and the Mac's own **System audio**. Each is recorded separately so they can be mixed afterwards.

![The Audio panel: include and volume for the microphone and for system audio](/docs/inspector-audio.webp)

For each source:

- **Include** puts it in the export or leaves it out.
- **Volume** sets its level.

The panel is offered for recordings that have an audio track or something to make sound from, and it says so when there is neither.

## Typing and click sounds

A screen recording of typing is usually silent, or has a room microphone making the keyboard sound worse than it does. Prequel can make the sounds instead, from the moments you typed and clicked, so they land on the keystrokes you see.

Under **Sounds** in the Audio panel:

- **Keyboard** picks the switch you hear — **Tactile**, **Linear**, **Clicky** or **Thock** — or **iPhone**, the soft tock of a phone keyboard, or **Off**. Choosing one plays it.
- **Mouse clicks** picks **Soft** or **Mechanical**, or **Off**.
- **Volume** sets each, like the tracks above.

New projects start with Tactile and Soft on. Like every setting in the panel, a choice applies to the whole edit or to one clip, so a clip can have a different keyboard or none.

The sounds are synthesised, not recorded: there is no sample library, and the preview and the export make the same sounds from the same plan. The space bar, Return and Delete sound bigger than a letter, as they do on a real board, and no two presses sound quite the same.

What the recording keeps to make this possible is the moment of each press and one of five classes — letter, space, Return, Delete, modifier — and never which letter. The switch for it is **Note when keys are pressed** in [Settings](/docs/settings), on by default. Passwords are never noted: macOS keeps them from every app while you type one. A take made with the switch off, or before it existed, offers click sounds alone.

## Recording system audio

Whether the Mac's sound is captured at all is a recording-time setting: **Record system audio** in Settings, on by default. A take made with it off has no system track to include later.

## GIF

A GIF export is silent whatever is set here. The export dialog says so in its summary line.
