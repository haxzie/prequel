## The four permissions

macOS asks for each of these separately, and Prequel asks only for what you are about to use.

![The permissions step of the welcome window, with Screen Recording allowed and three still to grant](/docs/welcome-permissions.webp)

| Permission | What it is for | Without it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Screen Recording | Everything Prequel records comes through it. | Nothing can be recorded at all. |
| Accessibility | Lets the automatic zooms find your clicks and typing, and gives the typing and click sounds their timing. | Clicks and typing are not captured, so automatic zooms have nothing to find and there are no sounds to make. |
| Camera | The webcam bubble, only when you turn the camera on. | Your camera is switched on but does not appear in the recording. |
| Microphone | Your voice, only when you turn the microphone on. | Your microphone is switched on but is not heard. |

Prequel never opens the camera or the microphone unless you turn them on for a recording.

## Granting them

The welcome window lists all four with an **Allow** button beside each. Each button opens the matching pane in System Settings, or triggers the system prompt where macOS offers one. The same list is in **Settings** under **Permissions**, so you can come back to it at any time.

## Screen Recording and Accessibility need a relaunch

macOS tells an app about these two grants only when it launches. If you allow Screen Recording or Accessibility while Prequel is running, the app still believes it is missing until you restart it.

The dock knows this. When something a recording needs is missing, an amber warning appears at the right end of the dock. Its menu lists what to allow and ends with **Restart Prequel**, which relaunches the app so it can read the new grant.

![The dock with an amber warning at its right end, because Accessibility has not been allowed](/docs/dock-permissions.webp)

## What "missing" means

The warning appears only for a permission the next recording would actually use. A camera that is switched off needs no camera grant, so the dock does not complain about one.
