## The dialog

**Export** in the editor's header opens the dialog.

![The export dialog: type, quality, frame rate and a summary line](/docs/export-dialog.webp)

- **Type**: **MP4** (H.264, plays everywhere), **HEVC** (smaller at the same quality, less widely playable) or **GIF** (silent, looping, and much larger per second).
- **Quality**: **Full** keeps the frame's own size. **1080p**, **720p** and **480p** scale it so the shorter edge is that many pixels. A GIF is capped at 720.
- **Frame rate**: 60 or 30 for MP4 and HEVC; 20 or 10 for GIF.

The line under the controls sums it up, for instance **1920 × 1080 · 60 fps**, with **silent** added for a GIF.

## Rendering

Click **Export** and choose where to save; the dialog opens on Downloads the first time and remembers where you last saved. The export runs on the Mac's video hardware, and a ring shows progress. You can keep editing meanwhile, and closing the dialog does not stop it; the **Export** button brings it back where it left off.

## When it is done

![The export dialog after a render: a looping preview, Reveal in Folder, Copy and Share Link](/docs/export-done.webp)

The finished video plays back in the dialog.

- **Drag and drop anywhere**: drag the preview into Slack, Mail, a browser or a folder.
- **Reveal in Folder** shows the file in Finder.
- **Copy** puts the file on the clipboard, ready to paste into a message or a folder.
- **Share Link** uploads it and gives you a link. See [Share links](/docs/share-links).

Exports need a trial or a paid licence; see [Pricing and trial](/docs/pricing-and-trial).
