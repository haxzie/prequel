## Reading the timeline

The ruler runs along the top; click anywhere on it to move the playhead. Below it, the clip row shows the recording as a filmstrip with the audio's waveform over it. A fresh recording is one clip. Under the clips, the zoom row holds the zooms. See [Zooms](/docs/zooms).

Hold **Command** and scroll, or pinch on the trackpad, to zoom the timeline in and out.

## Playback

**Space** plays and pauses. The transport also has **Go to start** and **Go to end**, and the time shows the playhead against the edited length, which shrinks as you cut.

![The transport: time, playback, and the add zoom, undo, split and delete verbs](/docs/playback-controls.webp)

## Trimming

Drag either end of a clip to trim it. The filmstrip moves with the edge so you can see the frame you are cutting to.

## Splitting and removing

1. Move the playhead to where the cut should be.
2. Press **S**, or click the scissors, to split the clip there.
3. Click the part you want gone and press **Backspace**.

The clips either side close up, and the finished video runs straight through. Right-clicking a clip offers **Split here** at the pointer and **Delete clip**.

## Cutting by the words

If the recording has captions, the caption editor is another way to cut: select a run of words and press Backspace, and the footage they were spoken over is removed. See [Captions](/docs/captions).

## Undo

**Command + Z** undoes any of this.
