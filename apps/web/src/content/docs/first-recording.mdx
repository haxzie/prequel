## Start

Press **Shift + Command + R**, or click the Prequel icon in the menu bar. The dock appears at the bottom of the screen.

1. Choose what to record: **Entire screen**, **Window** or **Area**. See [Sources](/docs/sources).
2. Turn the camera and microphone on or off with the two toggles. See [Camera and microphone](/docs/camera-and-microphone).
3. Pick your source in the overlay that appears over the screen, then click **Start recording**.

A countdown runs over the region you chose, three seconds by default, and the take begins.

## While recording

The dock collapses to a small bar with the elapsed time, a pause button and a stop button. The menu bar icon shows the elapsed time as well, so the dock can be hidden without losing track.

![The dock while recording: a timer, discard, pause and stop](/docs/dock-recording.webp)

- **Shift + Command + P** pauses and resumes without ending the take. Paused time is dropped from the recording.
- The bin discards the take entirely.

## Stop

Press **Shift + Command + R** again, or click the stop button. One key does both jobs: it begins a recording and stops the one already running.

The editor opens on what you just recorded, with the camera framed over the screen against a background. If Accessibility was allowed, a first pass has already added zooms wherever you clicked or typed.

![The editor, open on a fresh recording](/docs/editor.webp)

From here, [The editor](/docs/editor-overview) explains what is on screen, and [Export](/docs/export) is how the file leaves the app.
