## I allowed Screen Recording but Prequel still says it is missing

macOS reports Screen Recording and Accessibility to an app only when it launches. Quit Prequel from the menu bar and open it again, or use **Restart Prequel** in the dock's warning menu. See [Permissions](/docs/permissions).

## Nothing is recorded, or the recording is black

Screen Recording has not been granted, or was granted after launch. Relaunch, then check **Settings › Permissions**.

## No automatic zooms

Automatic zooms come from your clicks and typing, which the recording can only see with the Accessibility permission granted before the take. Recordings made without it have nothing for the wand to work from, and the editor says so when you hover it.

## The camera or microphone shows a red dot

The device is switched on in Prequel but cannot be opened. It may have been unplugged, or another app may be holding it. Choose it again from the chevron menu, or pick another device.

## A recording says it could not be opened

The editor shows this when a recording's folder is no longer where it was. It may have been deleted or moved out of the recordings folder. See [Where files live](/docs/where-files-live).

## Captions never appear

Transcription needs a microphone track and, the first time, a download of a speech model from Apple. Give it a moment with the Captions panel open. A recording with nothing said in it ends on **No speech to caption**.

## Finding the log

Right-click the menu bar icon and choose **Show Log in Finder**. The file is `~/Library/Logs/Prequel/main.log`, and it records app lifecycle, export progress and any errors. Attach it when you write to [support@prequel.sh](mailto:support@prequel.sh).

## Quitting

There is no Dock icon, so the menu bar is the way out: right-click the icon and choose **Quit Prequel**.
