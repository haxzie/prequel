Loom now has two ways to cancel, and picking the wrong one is why so many people end up looking at a button that is not there. This page was checked against Loom's and Atlassian's documentation on 5 October 2026. Loom changed the flow in May 2026, so if a label has moved, trust the screen over this page.

## Which kind of account you have

Open your Loom workspace settings. If the page says "Loom is now a part of Atlassian. Manage settings and users in Atlassian Administration", your subscription is managed in Atlassian and you follow the first set of steps below.

Loom says you are in Atlassian if you signed up after February 2026, or if your older account has been moved over. A second tell is on the account settings page, which says "Your Profile is linked to Atlassian".

If neither message is there, you are on a legacy Loom account and the second set of steps applies.

## If Atlassian bills you

Loom's documentation says only Atlassian billing admins and Atlassian organisation admins can cancel an Atlassian-managed Loom subscription. That is the "you do not have permission" error people post about. If you are not an admin, someone who is has to do the steps.

1. Go to Atlassian Administration and open **Billing**, then **Subscriptions**.
2. Find Loom and choose **Manage**. If you have more than one, note your Transaction Account ID so you pick the right one.
3. Choose **Manage** on the right entitlement and select the Loom subscription.
4. Open the actions menu and choose **Deactivate**, then give a reason and continue.
5. Choose **Downgrade** to move to the free Starter plan, or **Continue** to cancel, then **Cancel subscription**.

You are then shown the exact end date.

**If the subscription is not there.** Atlassian has two versions of its billing screens, original and improved. If **Overview** is first in the left-hand menu you are on the original one, and the route is different: go to `my.atlassian.com`, expand the subscription and choose **Cancel Subscription**. This is the most likely reason people say Atlassian does not show their Loom plan.

Atlassian's own terms add three things. A cancelled subscription is deactivated 15 days after the end of the current period. No credit is offered for cancelling before the end of a billing cycle. Undoing a deactivation means contacting support. Enterprise plans cancel only by email to Atlassian.

## If you are on a legacy Loom account

1. In Loom, go to workspace settings and open **Plan and Billing**.
2. Choose **Manage Subscription**, then **Downgrade to Starter**.
3. Give a reason and choose **Complete cancellation**.

Loom says you keep the premium features until the end of the billing cycle and are not charged again at renewal. On a Business + AI trial there is no Manage Subscription button, because there is nothing to cancel.

## Remove paid seats first

If your bill went up because free Creator Lite seats were converted, cancelling the plan is not the only fix and not always the right one. A downgrade takes effect at the next renewal date. Deactivating a user is immediate. The details are in [why your Loom bill went up](/articles/loom-bill-went-up).

## What you lose, and what you keep

- **Access:** premium features run to the end of the billing cycle. There is no prorated refund.
- **Videos:** kept. Over 25, you can watch and share them but not record new ones until you delete down or upgrade.
- **Limits:** recordings drop to five minutes.
- **Branding:** custom branding goes, and every share page returns to the default.
- **Single sign-on:** it is switched off. If you had no password before SSO was enabled, you have to reset it, which is how a team can lock itself out.
- **Embeds:** Loom's documentation does not say what happens to a video embedded elsewhere, so test one before you cancel.

## Download before you downgrade

Loom only allows downloads on its two top paid tiers. Once you have dropped to Starter, getting a file out is harder, so do it first. [How to download a Loom video as an MP4](/articles/download-loom-video-mp4) has the detail.

## Refunds

Loom's billing policy states the no-refund position three times: for cancelling, for downgrading a member, and for deactivating one, where "all unused time for that user is forfeited". On annual plans, "no refund or credit is processed".

Cancelling before the renewal date is therefore the protection Loom gives you. Other tools differ: [Screen Studio](/articles/how-to-cancel-screen-studio) refunds nothing, and [Tella](/articles/how-to-cancel-tella) refunds a first purchase within 14 days. [The cancel button in every screen recorder](/articles/how-to-cancel-a-screen-recorder-subscription) puts them side by side.
