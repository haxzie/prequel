The Download button is missing from your Loom video because of your plan, not because of anything you did. This is who can download, what to do if you cannot, and how to get a file you own from now on.

## Who can download

Loom's help page is specific: "Downloads are only available to admins, creators part of a Business or Business + AI workspace, and admins, members part of an Enterprise workspace."

That leaves the free Starter plan and anything below Business without a download button. If you are one of those, the control is not greyed out by mistake.

## If you are allowed to download

Loom prepares the file after processing, and its page for this error says it can take around 30 minutes. If you see "Your video is not available for download yet", wait and try again. If the video itself is stuck, that is a different problem and [Loom stuck in processing](/articles/loom-stuck-in-processing) is the page for it.

## If you are not

You have three honest routes.

1. **Upgrade for one billing period, download everything, then cancel.** Loom says you keep premium features to the end of the billing cycle, so the cost is one period. Follow [how to cancel Loom](/articles/how-to-cancel-loom) and cancel before the renewal date, because Loom does not refund unused time.
2. **Ask whoever administers the workspace.** An admin on a paid plan can download.
3. **Re-record.** For a short video this is quicker than the other two.

Do the download before you downgrade. After the plan changes, the option goes with it. The free-plan limits that apply afterwards are in [Loom's free plan limits](/articles/loom-free-plan-limits), and the reason many people are in this position at all is in [why your Loom bill went up](/articles/loom-bill-went-up).

## Own the file from the start

Having a file you cannot export is a property of how a recorder is built. If the video lives on the provider's server, the provider decides when you can have it.

Prequel records to your Mac and exports an MP4 to wherever you point the save dialog. The finished video plays in the export dialog, and you can drag it into Slack, Mail or a folder, reveal it in Finder, or copy it to the clipboard. Exports to your own Mac are never counted against anything, never expire and have no limit. See [export](/docs/export) and [where files live](/docs/where-files-live).
