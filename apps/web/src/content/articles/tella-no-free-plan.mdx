If a comparison post told you Tella has a free tier, it was written for an older Tella. There is none now.

## What Tella says

Tella's help centre: "Tella has two paid plans, Pro and Premium, billed per user, and every new workspace starts with a 7-day free trial. There is no forever-free plan."

Its pricing page, checked on 5 October 2026, shows:

| Plan       | Price                | Billing         | What it adds                                                                                                                           |
| ---------- | -------------------- | --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pro        | $13 per user a month | Billed annually | Unlimited videos, export in 4K, analytics, team collaboration                                                                          |
| Premium    | $19 per user a month | Billed annually | Everything in Pro, plus 60 fps export, custom domain, domain restriction, removing Tella branding from video pages, advanced analytics |
| Enterprise | Custom               | Custom          | SSO and SCIM, a shared Slack channel, volume discounts                                                                                 |

The page has a monthly option, and the annual one is marked 50% off, so billing monthly costs more than the figures above. Read the toggle before you buy.

## The trial

Tella's help centre says trial exports are HD at 30 fps, and trial clips are capped at 2 hours. It also says uploading your own footage is for paid plans, with a 5 GB cap per file.

After seven days from creating the account, you can still view and share your videos, but you cannot record, edit or export until you pay. That last point matters, and it is why [how to cancel Tella](/articles/how-to-cancel-tella) says to export before you cancel.

## Why older reviews mention a free tier

Third-party reviews from before the change describe a free plan with a five-minute limit and a library cap. That is gone. If a post you are reading mentions it, check its date. Tella's own page is the source of truth, and its comparison table, as of today, is where the current limits live.

Tella does not put a watermark on exports at any point, according to its help centre. The limits are on resolution, frame rate and clip length during the trial.

## Prequel's trial

Prequel also has no free tier. It has a 7-day trial with the whole app in it, the same app on both plans, with no watermark and no length cap. After that it is $9 a month or $39 once.
