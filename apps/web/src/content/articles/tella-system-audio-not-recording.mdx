You recorded a demo with a video playing, or a call, and the file has your voice and no computer sound. In Tella, that depends on which Tella you used.

## What Tella documents

Tella's help centre has a page called "Record system audio (desktop sound)", and its answer differs by app.

- **Mac app:** system audio is on by default.
- **Windows app:** it is off by default, and the control is in settings, not on the control bar.
- **Web app and Chrome extension:** they can only capture the audio of a single Chrome tab.

The key sentence is: "The 'Also share tab audio' option only appears when sharing a browser tab." If you share a window or a whole display from the browser, there is no desktop sound at all.

## What to do in Tella

1. **Use the Mac app** if you want the Mac's sound.
2. **If you must use the browser,** share the browser tab that is making the sound and tick the option to share its audio.
3. **On Windows,** turn system audio on in settings before you record.
4. **Do a 10-second test** and play it back with the sound up.

## The Mac's own tools

QuickTime Player records the microphone. It does not record the Mac's own sound without extra software, which is why people install a virtual audio driver. [How to record your Mac screen with internal audio](/blog/record-mac-screen-with-internal-audio) goes through the options, and the [system audio](/glossary/system-audio) entry defines the term.

## Prequel records the Mac's sound on its own

Prequel records system audio as a separate track, with no virtual audio driver. **Record system audio** in Settings is on by default. In the editor, the **Audio** panel gives the microphone and the system audio each an **Include** switch and a **Volume**, so you can drop a video's soundtrack and keep your voice, or the reverse.

![The Prequel Audio panel, with Include and Volume for the microphone and system audio](/articles/prequel/audio-panel.webp)

A take made with system audio off has no system track to include later, so check the setting before an important recording. The [audio page](/docs/audio) and the [camera and microphone page](/docs/camera-and-microphone) list every control.
