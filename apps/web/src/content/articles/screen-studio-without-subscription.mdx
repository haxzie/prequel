Screen Studio is a subscription now, with no free tier and no way to export without paying. That surprises people after they have done the work, so here is what each way out costs.

## What it costs

Screen Studio charges $29 a month billed monthly, or $9 a month billed yearly, which is $108 a year. Every feature is in both. There is no free plan and, for new customers, no one-time licence. These are the prices on its own site as of 5 October 2026.

## Your four options

### 1. Pay for one month, export, cancel

For a single project this is the cheapest route: $29 for a month on the monthly plan, export everything you need, and cancel before it renews. Screen Studio's terms say the fees are non-refundable, so cancelling in time is the only protection. [How to cancel Screen Studio](/articles/how-to-cancel-screen-studio) has the route.

### 2. Pay yearly

$108 up front for a year at $9 a month. It is the lower price if you make recordings all year.

### 3. Keep an old licence

If you bought a one-time licence in the past, Screen Studio says it is still valid. What lapses is access to the latest updates. [Screen Studio's lifetime licence is one year of updates](/articles/screen-studio-lifetime-licence) explains what people found when theirs ran out.

### 4. Move to something you can buy once

A Screen Studio project is Screen Studio's. Prequel does not open another recorder's project files. It imports a video you already exported, with **Add Clip**, then **Import Video**. For new recordings, you can record in Prequel from the start.

Prequel costs $9 a month, or $39 once, after a 7-day free trial with the whole app in it. The lifetime licence has nothing to renew. That is what the [pricing page](/pricing) says today, and it is not a promise about tomorrow.

## Other tools make a different trade

A free tier usually comes with a limit. [Loom's free plan limits](/articles/loom-free-plan-limits) are five minutes and 25 videos, and [Tella has no free plan at all](/articles/tella-no-free-plan). [Screen Charm sells a lifetime licence too](/articles/screencharm-subscription-refund), at $79.
