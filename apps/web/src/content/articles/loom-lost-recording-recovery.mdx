You recorded ten minutes, pressed stop, and Loom either crashed or never produced a video. What you can get back depends on which Loom you used, and Loom's help pages are blunt about it.

Everything below is from those pages, last updated between April and September 2026.

## What Loom says about how often this happens

"In the event that Loom crashes during recording or your video is stuck in processing, we are very sorry! This currently happens to less than 0.1% of all videos." That is Loom's own figure.

Users describe the same thing from the other side. One person on r/techsupport wrote up the problems they kept hitting with the desktop app on a MacBook Air, and said Loom support had classed them as ongoing bugs with no fix.

## What can be recovered

- **Desktop app, Mac or Windows:** possibly. The app stores video parts on your computer while it records.
- **Chrome extension:** Loom says "we do not have a recovery method for these videos at the moment."
- **Mobile apps:** the same.

## Recover a desktop recording, in order

Loom's steps, in the order it gives them:

1. **Wait.** Give it at least 30 minutes and do not close the tab. A failed preview is filtered out of your dashboard after 24 hours.
2. **Restart the app.** Fully quit Loom from the menu bar, relaunch it and wait a few minutes. Its auto-recovery tries to upload any parts it still has.
3. **Try the recovery page.** Go to `loom.com/recover-videos` and paste the video ID, which is the end of the link: `loom.com/share/<video_id>`. It only works for videos you own that were recorded with the desktop app.
4. **Look on disk.** In Finder, choose **Go**, then **Go to Folder**, and paste `~/Movies/Loom/Temporary/`. Open the folder whose name matches the video ID.
5. **Send it to Loom.** Zip the whole folder, upload the zip to cloud storage that anyone with the link can view, and send the video ID and the zip link to Support at `loom.com/contact`.

If that folder is empty, Loom says Support cannot rebuild the recording from files on your computer. Do not delete the video from your library while Support looks at it.

## Lowering the risk

Loom recommends at least 5 Mbps of upload speed and an up-to-date app, and suggests restarting the device if a recording fails. It also says a dropout in your connection during recording or processing can stop a video from uploading.

The deeper fix is a recorder that does not depend on the connection at all. Two related pages cover the other half of this problem: [Loom stuck in processing](/articles/loom-stuck-in-processing) and [why a Loom recording is not the quality you set](/articles/loom-video-quality).

The same failure exists elsewhere. [Cap crashed mid-recording](/articles/cap-crash-lost-recording) is the open-source version of it, and [Tella's browser storage limits](/articles/tella-recording-storage-limits) is the cloud-first one. If the reason you cannot get into Loom to try any of this is the account, [Loom login since the Atlassian move](/articles/loom-login-atlassian) has the fix.
