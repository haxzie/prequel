Cap is an open-source recorder, which means its problems are public and numbered. The one about memory has been open for two years.

## The oldest open issue

In October 2024 Cap's own maintainer filed issue 109, "Reduce CPU / memory usage whilst recording", and attached a $350 bounty to it. Its text says some users are reporting memory creeping to 5 GB or more in longer recordings, from about two minutes.

![GitHub issue 109 on CapSoftware/Cap, Reduce CPU / memory usage whilst recording, open with 11 comments](/blog/quotes/cap-109-cpu-memory.webp)

[Read issue 109 on GitHub](https://github.com/CapSoftware/Cap/issues/109)

In the thread he said he had reproduced it on an iMac with 8 GB of memory: recording with a webcam and microphone, about two minutes in, memory started to rise until it passed 8 GB and the app crashed. Others had reported it on M1 and M2 Macs across macOS 14 and 15. A user on a MacBook Pro M4 with 16 GB wrote in May 2025 that Cap could take all of their RAM and then crash. A contributor submitted a pull request for the bounty in May 2026.

As of 5 October 2026 the issue is open.

## The related reports that were closed

Three other issues describe the same family of problem. All three were closed in August 2026 by the maintainer, who said each was believed resolved and asked for a re-test on the latest version.

- **Issue 586, "Laggy system while recording".** A user recording a 4K screen with a 1080p webcam saw the CPU at 100% with the GPU unused. A developer replied in 2025 that Cap encodes display video through VTEncoder in AVFoundation because it should be hardware accelerated, and that 4K might need different configuration. The maintainer closed it saying hardware encoder usage had been substantially reworked.
- **Issue 988, "Using all available CPU" on an M4 Max.** Closed with the same note about recording performance on macOS being reworked.
- **Issue 1673, "Cap heats up a lot".** The reporter said a laptop with camera and microphone on was noticeably warm within a few minutes, even in recordings of three to ten minutes. It was closed because there was no version or log to work from, with an invitation to re-file.

So the honest summary is that the CPU and heat reports were handled by a rework, and the memory one has not been closed. A thread on r/opensource shows how it reads from outside: someone looking for a Tella-style recorder says they tried Cap and found it pretty unstable.

## Check your own recording

Do not rely on a thread, which may be a year old. Take two minutes.

1. Open **Activity Monitor** and the **Memory** tab.
2. Start a recording with the camera and microphone on, as you would normally.
3. Watch the recorder and any helper processes for five minutes.
4. If memory climbs steadily and never levels off, stop and update the app. If it still climbs, file an issue with your version, macOS version and logs. The maintainers ask for those every time.
