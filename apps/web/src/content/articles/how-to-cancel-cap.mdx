Cap is open source, but it sells things, and its own documentation has nothing on how to stop paying. This page says what is stated and marks what is not. It was checked on 5 October 2026 against Cap's own site and terms.

## What Cap sells

- **Free:** personal use only.
- **Desktop Licence:** $29 a year per licence, or $58 once for a licence that never renews.
- **Cap Pro:** $12 per user a month, or $8.16 a month billed annually.
- **Add-ons:** SAML SSO at $199 a month and a signed BAA at $99 a month, both self-serve.

Dropping to free loses commercial usage rights, not only the cloud features. If you record for clients or a company, free is not a fallback.

## Where to cancel

The pricing page says "Cancel anytime". It does not say how, and Cap's documentation covers installation, the quickstart, agents, self-hosting and the commercial licence, but has no page on billing or cancelling.

The one billing address Cap states is the organisation settings in your dashboard, which it labels for managing add-ons. Start there, and look for the plan or subscription controls. I could not verify the menu labels beyond that, so I will not name buttons. If you cannot find a cancel control, email `hello@cap.so`.

## What happens to your recordings

Cap's own wording is the clearest part: "Your recordings are yours forever. If you cancel Pro, existing shares remain active and you can always export everything. Downgrade to our free plan to keep recording locally, or self-host to maintain all features."

Cap does not say whether cancelling takes effect immediately or at the end of the period. [The cancel button in every screen recorder](/articles/how-to-cancel-a-screen-recorder-subscription) compares what each tool does with your videos.

## Refunds

There is no refund policy in the terms. They were last updated on 24 April 2024, which is before the current pricing existed, and they contain no billing, cancellation or refund clause. The only termination wording runs the other way: Cap may terminate or suspend your access at any time, with or without cause or notice.

So the promises on the pricing page are the only ones. If a refund matters, ask in writing before you pay, and keep the reply.

- The shareable link on the free plan and the Desktop Licence is limited to five minutes, and only Pro lifts it. [The best Cap alternatives for Mac](/blog/cap-alternatives) goes through it.
