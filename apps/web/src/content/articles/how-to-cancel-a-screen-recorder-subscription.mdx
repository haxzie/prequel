## The one rule: export first

Three of these five keep your videos but take away the way to get a file out. Tella keeps them viewable and shareable and stops export. Loom allows downloads only on its top paid tiers, and a downgrade moves you off them. Screen Studio ends your right to use the app, which is the thing that exports.

So before you cancel anything, export every video you want as a file. [How to download a Loom video as an MP4](/articles/download-loom-video-mp4) covers the tool where this is hardest.

## The traps

- **Cancelling in the wrong place.** Loom has two routes and Atlassian has two billing screens. [How to cancel Loom](/articles/how-to-cancel-loom) says how to tell which you are on.
- **Not being the owner.** Loom wants an Atlassian billing or organisation admin. Tella wants the workspace owner.
- **Seats, not plans.** On Loom the bill can rise because free seats were converted. Removing users stops that, and downgrading does not in time: see [why your Loom bill went up](/articles/loom-bill-went-up).
- **A second login.** Tella says two logins cannot be merged and each workspace bills separately: [can't find your Tella recording](/articles/tella-cant-find-recording).
- **A lookalike site.** A different site on screenstudio.net advertises a refund Screen Studio does not offer.
- **Waiting.** With no refund, cancelling the day before renewal is the whole protection.

## A checklist

1. Export everything you need as files.
2. Find which account and which workspace is billing you.
3. Cancel before the renewal date.
4. Keep the confirmation email and note the end date.
5. Check the next statement.
