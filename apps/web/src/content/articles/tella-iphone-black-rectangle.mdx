You mirrored your iPhone to the Mac to record a demo, started the recording, and got a black rectangle where the phone should be. Tella documents it. The fix works in any recorder.

## What Tella says

On its tip page for iPhone demos, Tella's help centre says "AirPlay mirroring records as a black rectangle". The route it gives is to connect the device by cable and record a QuickTime Player window instead.

## Show the phone through a cable

This is how the Mac's own QuickTime Player does it.

1. Connect the iPhone to the Mac with a cable and unlock it. Tap **Trust** if it asks.
2. Open **QuickTime Player** and choose **File**, then **New Movie Recording**.
3. Click the small arrow beside the record button.
4. Under **Camera**, choose your iPhone. The phone's screen appears in the window.
   You now have a normal Mac window showing the phone. A normal window is the thing screen recorders can record.

## Record that window

Do not use QuickTime's own record button for the demo unless you want a plain file. Record the window with a recorder that can zoom and frame it.

In Prequel, choose **Window** on the dock. The screen dims and the window under your pointer is highlighted with its app icon and name. Click the QuickTime window, then press **Start recording**. See [sources](/docs/sources).

In the editor you can place the phone on a background, add zooms, and add your own camera in a corner with one of the layouts: see [layout](/docs/layout) and [zooms](/docs/zooms).

## Making the phone look right

- **Turn on Do Not Disturb** on the phone first, so no banner crosses the demo.
- **Resize the QuickTime window** to the phone's proportions before you record, so the frame is not mostly empty.
- **Check the sound** with a 10-second test. The iPhone's microphone and the Mac's are different devices, and [why screen recording audio drifts out of sync](/articles/screen-recording-audio-out-of-sync) explains what happens when two devices disagree.

A related tool-specific limit is [Tella is not recording computer audio](/articles/tella-system-audio-not-recording). For the limits on what Tella lets you change afterwards, see [what Tella's editor can't do after you record](/articles/tella-editor-limits), and for audio sync, [Tella audio out of sync](/articles/tella-audio-out-of-sync).
