You recorded something, and now you cannot open it, or cannot see it at all. Tella's help centre has two separate pages for these, and both come from how the product is organised.

## It will not open for editing

Tella: "If a video you just recorded on the Tella Mac app won't open for editing, the Mac app and your browser are usually signed in to different Tella accounts. Compare the two email addresses and, if they differ, sign in to the browser with the one the Mac app uses."

This follows from the design. The app records and the website edits, and they sign in separately. [Why Tella makes you wait before you can edit](/articles/tella-cloud-editing) explains the order of events.

## It is not in your library

A second page is titled "Where is the video I just recorded?". The answer is that videos belong to a workspace, and a recording goes into whichever workspace was active when you pressed record. If you have a personal workspace and a work one, your library can look empty because you are in the wrong one.

## Moving a video

Tella says: "No. There's no button to move a single video to another workspace." The route is to download the video and upload it again. Tella warns: "The upload is a fresh video: edits, layouts, and analytics don't carry over." For more than a few videos, you ask support to migrate them.

## Merging accounts

"Can I merge two Tella accounts? No. Two Tella logins (e.g. a Google login and an email login for the same person) can't be merged automatically, and each workspace bills separately."

That last clause means a second login can mean a second subscription. If you are looking at your bill, [how to cancel Tella](/articles/how-to-cancel-tella) covers the route and [Tella has no free plan](/articles/tella-no-free-plan) lists what each plan costs.

## What to check, in order

1. Are the Mac app and the browser signed in with the same email?
2. Which workspace is active in each?
3. Is the upload finished? Do not delete the app's local folder while it uploads: see [Tella stopped recording](/articles/tella-recording-storage-limits).
4. Can the editor load at all? [Tella editor won't load](/articles/tella-editor-wont-load) lists the four errors.
5. If the editor loads but is limited, [what Tella's editor can't do after you record](/articles/tella-editor-limits) is the page.

For a wider comparison, see the [Tella page](/alternatives/tella).

## Every take is a folder

In Prequel, a recording is a folder in `~/Movies/Prequel`. There is no account to mismatch and no workspace to pick, because recording, editing and export do not need one. **Show Recordings in Finder** in the menu bar opens the folder, and every take is also listed in the library inside the app.

A folder is self-contained, so you can copy it to another Mac with Prequel and it opens the same. Signing in exists for sharing and for the licence. See [where files live](/docs/where-files-live) and [share links](/docs/share-links).
