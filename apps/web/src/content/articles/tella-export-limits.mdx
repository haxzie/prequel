Tella's export options depend on the plan and on whether you are still on the trial. This page puts them together from Tella's own pricing page and help centre, checked on 5 October 2026.

## By plan

|                                        | Trial           | Pro                                   | Premium                               |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Price                                  | Free for 7 days | $13 per user a month, billed annually | $19 per user a month, billed annually |
| Recording resolution                   | Up to 4K        | Up to 4K                              | Up to 4K                              |
| Export                                 | HD at 30 fps    | Export in 4K                          | Export in 4K, and at 60 fps           |
| Longest clip                           | 2 hours         | 6 hours                               | 6 hours                               |
| Remove Tella branding from video pages | No              | No                                    | Yes                                   |

The pricing page offers a monthly option beside the annual one and marks annual as 50% off, so billing monthly costs more than the figures above.

## What the table does not spell out

Tella's comparison table shows "Up to 5 mins" beside two of its export rows. In the text the page does not say which plan or which export it applies to, so I will not guess. If 60 fps matters to you, read that table for the plan you are buying.

## Other limits

- **Clip length:** up to 6 hours per clip on every paid plan. Combine clips for longer. Trial clips are capped at 2 hours.
- **Uploading your own footage:** a paid-plan feature, with a 5 GB cap per file. Larger files need compressing first.
- **Cameras:** Tella supports one camera at a time.
- **Watermark:** Tella says it never puts its logo or a watermark on your videos and that exported files are always clean.

## What is not a limit

Nothing here is wrong with Tella. It is a per-user subscription, and the plan boundaries are the business model. They sit in places you only reach late: at the export, after the editing is done.

## Prequel's export

Prequel has no length cap on a recording and holds nothing back by plan: the trial, the subscription and the lifetime licence are the same app, with no watermark. **Export** offers MP4 (H.264), HEVC and GIF, and Full, 1080p, 720p or 480p, with the resulting size shown beside the choice.
