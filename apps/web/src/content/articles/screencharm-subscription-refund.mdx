There is nothing to cancel with Screen Charm, which is the answer most people searching for it want. This page says what its own terms and site state about the price and the refund, checked on 5 October 2026.

## What it costs

Screen Charm's terms of service, last updated on 31 January 2026, say: "The lifetime deal costs $79.00 (USD)... you will have unlimited access to video export features with no recurring fees."

Its site adds that you can use it on up to 3 devices, that updates are free for life, and that there is no subscription and no renewal. The free tier records, and exporting is what the licence unlocks.

## Cancelling

There is no subscription, so there is no cancel button and no renewal date to beat. If you are looking at a charge you do not recognise, it is not from Screen Charm's licence, which is a single payment.

## The refund

Its terms offer a 14-day refund: you can request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase, no questions asked. You request it by emailing the address on its terms page. That is a person's inbox, not a billing system, so keep the date of purchase and write early.

## Check your Mac first

Screen Charm runs only on Apple Silicon Macs, on macOS Sonoma 14.0 or later. A buyer on r/SideProject, in a thread where the founder introduced the app, wrote that they had bought it before even trying it and that their Mac was an older Intel model, so they could not use it. A reply in the thread said the refund had been made.

[Read the thread on r/SideProject](https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/comments/1m8tf60/i_built_a_macos_screen_recorder_with_smart_zoom/)

The same requirement applies to Prequel. Check About This Mac before you buy either.

## Prequel's one-off licence

Prequel's lifetime licence is $39 once. It is the whole app, with no watermark and no length cap, and it has nothing to renew and nothing to cancel. What it does not do is grow: it comes with 5 GB of shared recordings, each kept for three months. Exports to your own Mac are never counted and never expire. The pricing page also says the price might rise, and that today's price is not a promise about tomorrow. See [pricing](/pricing) and [what the trial includes](/docs/pricing-and-trial).
