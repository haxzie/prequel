If a Screen Studio export is taking longer than the recording itself, you are describing something other users describe too. This page is what they say, what decides export time in any Mac recorder, and what you can change today.

## What users report

A user on r/screenrecorders, making tutorial videos about accounting and AI, wrote in February 2026 that Screen Studio was so slow that for every 10 minutes of recording they spent about half an hour exporting, and that they could not stand the slowness or the occasional bugs.

[Read the thread on r/screenrecorders](https://www.reddit.com/r/screenrecorders/comments/1p55qvu/looking_for_a_free_video_recording_software_whats/)

That is one commenter and a thread about something else, so it is a report and not a benchmark. Export time matters because it is the part of a recorder you only meet at the end, after the work is done. The same pattern shows up for heat: [Why Screen Studio makes your MacBook run hot](/articles/screen-studio-overheating).

## What decides export time

Four things set how much work an export is, and a fifth decides how fast the Mac does it.

- **Length.** Twice the minutes, roughly twice the frames to render.
- **Size.** A 4K frame has four times the pixels of a 1080p one.
- **Frame rate.** 60 frames a second is twice the work of 30.
- **Effects.** Zoom, blur, motion and a framed background are drawn on every frame. A heavily edited clip costs more than a plain one.

The fifth is where the encoding happens. A Mac has a dedicated hardware video encoder, and an export that uses it finishes faster and leaves the processor cores free, which software encoding does not.

## What to change

1. **Export a review copy first.** A 1080p, 30 fps draft shows you what the final will look like in a fraction of the time. Do the full-size export once, at the end.
2. **Keep the Mac plugged in** and the lid open, so it does not throttle.
3. **Close other apps.** A browser with many tabs competes for the same memory.
4. **Split a long recording** into sections if the recorder lets you export a range.
