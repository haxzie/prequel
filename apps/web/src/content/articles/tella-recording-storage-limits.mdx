Tella has two different storage limits, one for the browser and one for the desktop apps, and both can end a recording early. They are in Tella's help centre, and worth knowing before a long take.

## Why storage matters at all

Tella's help centre says that when you record, it "temporarily saves the video file to your Mac's local storage before uploading it to the cloud." The disk has to hold the whole recording first. That is the cause of everything below. It is also part of why [Tella makes you wait before you can edit](/articles/tella-cloud-editing).

## The browser limit

Tella needs at least 2 GB of free browser storage to start recording, and "your browser can run out even when your disk has space." It has separate help pages titled "Not enough browser storage to record" and "Browser storage isn't working".

The browser's quota is not your disk. A Mac with 200 GB free can still fail this check.

## The desktop app limit

On the Mac and Windows apps, Tella says it "won't start a recording with less than 512 MB free and stops safely if free space drops below 256 MB; keep 5-10 GB free to be safe."

If it stops: "The recording will be shorter than planned, but Tella keeps and uploads the part recorded before it stopped."

One warning from the same page matters more than the numbers: "Don't delete anything in that folder while a video is still uploading. Those are the only copies of that recording."

## If a recording was lost

Tella says a recording that was cancelled, crashed or errored before saving can usually still be recovered by its support, with conditions: do not delete it, do not clear browser site data, keep the desktop app installed, and contact support quickly.

That is a recovery route, and it depends on act-fast conditions. The same shape exists in other tools: [Loom crashed and lost your recording](/articles/loom-lost-recording-recovery) has Loom's steps, and [Cap crashed mid-recording](/articles/cap-crash-lost-recording) has Cap's.

## Before a long take

1. Check free space against the 5 to 10 GB Tella recommends, more for 4K.
2. In a browser, check the storage quota too, not only the disk.
3. Do not clear site data or delete app folders until the upload finishes.
4. If a take is important, do a 30-second test first.

## Where Prequel keeps a take

Prequel keeps every take as a folder under `~/Movies/Prequel` on your Mac, and the folder holds the finished files: `screen.mp4`, `camera.mp4`, `mic.m4a` and `system.m4a`. Nothing has to upload before the recording is usable. **Show Recordings in Finder** in the menu bar opens the folder. See [where files live](/docs/where-files-live).
