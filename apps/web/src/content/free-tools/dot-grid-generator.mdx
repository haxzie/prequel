## A dot at every crossing

A dot grid is graph paper with the lines taken out, which is why a bullet
journal uses one: it gives you something to align to and then gets out of the
way. This page draws one at the size you ask for, with a bigger dot on every
fifth crossing both ways so you can still count a distance off it.

The Grid slider sets how many dots fit across the short edge, from 14 to 72. The
dots are sized against that edge too, so a dense grid has small dots and a
loose one has large ones, which is the proportion that keeps it readable.

## Light enough to put something on

Pick Carbon or Slate and the field reads as texture under a screenshot. Pick
Paper and it is a notebook page. Olive, Rose, Sand and Mint are the louder ones,
and the heavy dots in those are the colour the rulers are drawn in elsewhere in
this set.

## PNG or SVG

The PNG is painted in this tab at the size in the dock. The SVG is two paths of
zero-length strokes with round caps, which is one of the reasons a 6K field
draws quickly: a few thousand circle elements is a document a rasteriser spends
real time on, and this is not that.

## Behind a recording

Prequel ships this grid as one of seven drawn backgrounds in its editor, redrawn
at the export's own resolution. Record your Mac's screen, and the editor opens
with the zooms already placed, your camera framed and a background behind it.
