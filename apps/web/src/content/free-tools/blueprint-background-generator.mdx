## Drafting paper, not a photograph

A blueprint background is a flat ground, lines on a coarse pitch, a small cross
at every crossing and a double border round the sheet. This page draws all four
at the size you ask for, so the border sits the same distance in on a phone
wallpaper and on a 6K one.

The crosses are what make it read as drafting paper. Band lines alone are a
coarse grid; the crosses are the registration marks a drawing is set out
against, and they land on every fifth intersection in both directions.

## The blue, and seven other colours

Blueprint is the obvious palette and the default on this page is not the only
option. Carbon and Slate are the ones to use behind a screenshot of a dark
interface. Paper turns the same drawing into a sheet from a flat file, with the
lines darker than the ground.

## PNG or SVG

The PNG is painted in this tab, at the size in the dock, with no watermark and
no sign up. The SVG is the better file for anything that gets resized later: it
is three paths and two rectangles, and it scales to any size with the hairlines
intact.

## Behind a recording

Prequel ships this drawing as one of seven backgrounds in its editor, redrawn at
the export's own resolution. Record your Mac's screen, and the editor opens with
the zooms already placed, your camera framed and a background behind it.
