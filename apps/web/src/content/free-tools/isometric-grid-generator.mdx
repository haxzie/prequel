## Three directions, one grid

An isometric grid is uprights plus two families of lines at thirty degrees, and
the three together give you the axes of a box drawn in isometric projection.
Sketch on it and a cube takes one minute. Put it behind a screenshot and it
reads as depth without anything in the picture moving.

The lines run corner to corner across the whole frame, so there is no seam and
nothing tiles. The pitch is measured perpendicular to the lines, which is the
spacing your eye actually reads, and the Grid slider sets it against the frame's
short edge.

## Any shape of frame

Pick a 9:16 phone size and the grid is the same pitch it was in landscape, with
more lines crossing the frame. Nothing is stretched, because nothing is
resampled: the drawing is worked out again for the size you asked for.

## PNG or SVG

The PNG is painted here at the size in the dock and saved to your downloads
folder. The SVG is a single path, which makes it a few kilobytes and makes it
easy to recolour by hand in Figma or Illustrator afterwards.

## Behind a recording

Prequel ships this grid as one of seven drawn backgrounds in its editor, redrawn
at the export's own resolution. Record your Mac's screen, and the editor opens
with the zooms already placed, your camera framed and a background behind it.
