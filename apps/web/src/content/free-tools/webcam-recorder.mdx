## It asks what you are posting to

Pick the frame first: vertical 9:16 for a Reel or a Short, square for a feed,
4:5 for a post, landscape for everything else. The recording comes out at that
size, so there is nothing to reframe afterwards.

Your camera is cropped to the middle of its frame rather than squeezed into the
shape you picked. A 1280 by 720 webcam filling a 1080 by 1920 video is a crop
either way; cropping to the centre keeps your face where it was and keeps your
proportions right.

Mirror is on when the camera opens, because that is the version of your face you
know. Turn it off for anything with words in shot: text on a shirt, a whiteboard
behind you, a product label held up to the lens.

## Check the sound before the take

The level moves as soon as the microphone opens, before the countdown. It reads
the device in the picker and follows the level slider, so a microphone pointing
at the wrong thing shows up in the first second rather than at the end of ten
minutes.

Every camera and microphone your browser can see is in those pickers, including
a USB webcam, a capture card and an iPhone on Continuity Camera. Switch between
them with the preview running.

## What the Mac app adds

This page records one camera into one file. Prequel on a Mac records your camera
and your screen as separate tracks, so the shape, size and corner of your camera
are still yours when the take is finished, with a background behind it and the
zooms over your screen already placed.
