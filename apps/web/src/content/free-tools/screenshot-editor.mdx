## Paste, mark, copy

The fastest path through this page has no files in it. Take a screenshot with
Command Shift 4, paste it here with Command V, drag out an arrow, press Copy,
and paste the result into Slack. Nothing was saved to disk and nothing was
uploaded.

Your browser does all of it. The image is read with a FileReader, drawn into a
canvas on your machine, and read back out as a PNG when you ask for it. There is
no account, no watermark and no extension to install.

## The marks

Five pens, each sized against the image rather than set in pixels, so a mark on
a 5K capture is as visible as one on a small crop.

- **Arrow** points at the thing you are describing.
- **Rectangle** puts a box around it.
- **Highlighter** multiplies into the picture, so the words under it stay
  readable instead of going grey.
- **Redact** pixelates the region into blocks. The export is made from that
  canvas, so the original pixels are not in the file you download, which is not
  true of a blur.
- **Text** drops a label with a dark halo behind it, so it reads on a light
  screenshot and a dark one.

Select any mark to move it, recolour it or delete it. Command Z steps back.

## The ground

Six backgrounds from the Prequel palette, with padding, a corner radius and a
shadow under the picture. The screenshot itself is never scaled to fit: the
export is your original pixels with the padding around them, so small text comes
out as sharp as it was captured.

## When a still is not enough

A screenshot shows the state something was in. A recording shows how it got
there. Prequel records your Mac's screen as video, places the zooms on every
click, frames your camera and exports at up to 4K.
